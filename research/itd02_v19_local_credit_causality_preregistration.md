## Locked mechanism question
Does V18's reward-credit rule update intermediate receiving-cell gains when those gains had **zero causal influence on the selected action head's pre-update reward prediction**? Its current rule is `delta_gain ~ reward_error * reverse_credit * local incoming-activation`; it does not test a per-cell sensitivity/eligibility trace of the selected output to the gain. A gain can therefore change even if its true causal local derivative is zero.

**This is a source-only falsification/diagnostic, not a replacement learner and not original-world science.** V19 must not change a single V18 learner byte, encoder or source law. Use the identical external predeclared V18 additive and nonlinear toy target functions from its unchanged harness, never import targets into a candidate implementation.

Because original V18 head weights are exactly zero, **all 56 derivatives must be identically zero** to 1e-10; changing gains cannot change its output at initialization.
3. Control: same public trace with reward **0.0**, zero initial predicted error, must yield **zero gain and head update**. Include one invalid public reward and an attempt to change an unselected head, fail-closed without mutation.
