## Why the portfolio shifted
NCI-08 established a hard uncompressed relationship limit; NCI-09 showed coded span memory can resolve that finite limit. NCI-10 through R2 improved some native memory decisions but failed contradictory or shifted evidence. NCI-11 and R2 showed positive trained-memory contribution over zero-state while missing accepted prospective thresholds and losing to programmed compression. NCI-11 R3 introduced a large GRU and bilinear gating under the same 576-byte resident StatePool, but neither generalized across new graph identities and structures. More NCI assembly around weak learned abstractions is not the best next use of effort.

## Six foundational work credits, in priority order
1. **F1 — Context-dependent learned developmental memory:** train a native state update/selection mechanism using retained/local public state, relational roles and uncertainty, not hard-coded path tuples or future-query leakage. Preserve seven active/nine retained semantics where applicable.
2. **F2 — Equivariant binding and structural generalization:** learn roles and reusable relations under entity renaming, reversed edges, novel graph motifs, contradictory evidence and delayed queries. Require a same-architecture untrained control and permutation tests.
3. **F3 — Predictive retention and adaptive consolidation:** determine what to store or compress based on development-only future-question loss. Compare FIFO/LRU, coded NCI-09, previous learned policy and memory removal within actual physical state budgets.
4. **F4 — Learnable multi-timescale retained-state use:** test fixed-capacity fast/local versus retained development state with context-dependent routing, acquisition and forgetting; protect earlier knowledge under new tasks.
5. **F5 — Recovery and interruption causality:** deliberately interrupt and restore native state, compare exact resumed versus uninterrupted answers and update trajectories, quantify state-cost and persistence.
6. **F6 — Mechanism falsification and capacity impossibility:** determine whether improvements persist under contradictory updates, impossible simultaneous goals, geometry changes and new relation grammars; separate genuine learning from coded representations and biased labels.

**Next immediate gate:** conduct F1/F2 development-only architecture and baseline qualification with disjoint entity vocabularies; no new sealed first-use if learned update/readout cannot reliably beat same initialized state and the strongest resource-matched programmed reference.

## Three native cognition integration credits

3. **I3:** evaluate delayed information, checkpoint recovery, changing knowledge and old/new task interference in one bounded longitudinal cognitive cycle.

A passing workflow or a coded memory shortcut does not meet these criteria.
