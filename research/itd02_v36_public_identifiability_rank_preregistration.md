## Distinguish two information questions rather than confounding them

1. In the **narrow and publicly disclosed finite toy hypothesis set {D1,D2,D3} from V35**, can the actual originally selected twelve public outcomes uniquely label which existing synthetic table produced them, *without querying unselected rewards?* Compute the vector of original `selected_map()[0]` legal rewards for each law and count pairwise exact outcome collisions (1e-12 threshold); repeat after each separately chosen 3-extra-probe history. The finite family set is known **only to the evaluator**, never native model, RLS, or chooser. Finite-set distinguishability is NOT evidence that the learner has learned a world representation or that an arbitrary unfamiliar world is identifiable.
2. For the **broader reward-law class not limited to the three frozen toy mappings**, do the same selected 12+3 public rewards *uniquely* determine optimal second actions? Construct two offline-only total law completions **for each arm's real selected observations**: `W_LOW` exactly matches every observed (cue,actual_first,selected_second)->reward and assigns -3.0 to each never-selected second action; `W_HIGH` matches exactly the same observed rewards and assigns +9.0 to each never-selected second action. Both are legal scalar reward assignments in the original bounds, and are **never shown to a learner, chooser, policy, or checkpoint**. Their selected-history digests must be identical, and the evaluator compares optimal action vectors across 12 frozen cue×first contexts. This is an information insufficiency *witness for the unrestricted bounded table family*, not proof that the D1/D2/D3 family is ambiguous. If observed public action reward outside [-3,9], classify engineering blocked rather than clipping/repairing.
3. With the exact source and legal selected-feedback actions, did original V22 shared and the preexisting opt-in V35 candidate **actually move any of their 36 frozen action scores**, their 12 action rankings, or their offline regret? Measure before/after each model's 3 actual selected extra novelty probes, rank margins, score delta magnitude, chosen action changes, native 163-scalar SHA changes, selected-error improvement and complete paired conventional RLS controls. **Score movement without improving action rank/regret** is a concrete learning-to-decision mismatch; the narrow finite-family identifiability does not itself give the model a hidden family identifier.
