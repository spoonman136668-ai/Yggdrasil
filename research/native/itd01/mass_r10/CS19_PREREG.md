**Frozen five arms:**
1. `trained_rank_only_Laplace`: conventional online per-current-rank 13×2 genuine original rewarded observations of HIGHER versus LOWER outcomes (ignore 0 tie, Laplace +1/+1; choose higher if P(high)>=0.5, tie higher). Captures rank prior from exact FIT, no historical counts at DEV.
2. `trained_public_history_logistic`: equally original-FIT-trained logistic predictor with 13 current-rank one-hot plus learned **two public-history summary features** (remaining observed HIGH and LOW potential counts scaled by max 4×num_decks, and explicit public progress fraction), bias+13+3 weights, single fixed 12 chronological epochs SGD LR0.08 L2=0.003 on the **same** FIT rewarded source outcomes; if next observed rank ties, reward0→discard that FIT label only for every trained model that requires a binary direction. The high/low count features are a hand-authored source-position structure and **NOT a learned selection/gating principle**.
3. `trained_public_K3_hist_logistic`: same learner architecture, epochs and legitimate FIT outcomes, but may retain counts of **only the first 3 rank indices**, pretending unseen counts of the other 10 ranks remain at initial prior. It receives current rank and public time index, not a full hidden histogram; its own serialized original model state, total tracked counters/metadata must be reported. This is a conventional capped-memory information ablation, **not a qualified trainable native relevance gate**.
4. `source_known_full_remaining_oracle`: source-published original deck composition 4×num_decks cards per rank and ALL public prior ranks; choose higher iff real remaining HIGH ranks >= remaining LOW ranks, otherwise lower. No FIT consumed and cannot be compared as equally trained. This has full memory and source semantics; tie deterministic HIGH.
5. `source_known_memoryless_rank_prior`: identical deck published original rank law but no prior observed ranks: choose higher if initially more ranks above current than below, else lower. Privileged oracle-specific no-memory comparator with ZERO training, not a learned baseline.

FIT reward→binary direction labels recovered **only after actual source action and original returned reward**, never by looking ahead to next public observation for model fitting (next rank may be recorded as independent source audit AFTER source action, but not used to train any model).
