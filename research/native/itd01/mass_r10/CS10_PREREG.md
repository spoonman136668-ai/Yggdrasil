## Research hypothesis
Fit a six-weight logistic predictor of a cached public card rank's reappearance at a **different** public card position during the subsequent 24 observed legal actions. Its training labels are constructed exclusively from later observations in public FIT source trajectories, not privileged original hidden cards. Use the fitted probability to evict the predicted least useful card from a genuinely binding K4 cache. This differs from CS09's selection among handcrafted eviction rules, but the feature basis, source-public card matching planner and memory architecture are STILL handcrafted; this is supervised future-public-observation prediction, not reward-trained end-to-end native learning.
