## Exact question

NLD-04 identified the active task from eight public delayed probes **given a prelisted pair of A/B hypotheses** and two pre-existing cue-conditioned policies. Can a bounded learner instead **create context slots from contradiction in its own learned predictions**, and later select the appropriate slot from public feedback, without receiving task IDs or a prelisted A/B hypothesis set?

This tests *error-driven context slot formation*, not self-organizing causal abstractions. A source phase reset is public and may provide useful segmentation information. One task may be easier to infer than another. The original R9 A/B family laws are already exposed; no unseen transfer claim.
