## Falsifiable question

On a **prospectively identified, disjoint, source-native stream** of witnessed (public observation, legal action, subsequent next observation/reward/termination), can a small locally updated meta-Hebbian learner retain a familiar action-consequence model across change and autonomously recall it **after diagnostic public feedback**, while rejecting truly unfamiliar regimes, with lower prequential prediction loss and higher matched-action decision value than the strongest fully trained source-blind conventional comparator?

## Candidate mechanisms — exact implementations must be frozen separately before any original evaluation

1. **Local eligibility / three-factor plasticity**: bounded normalized pre- and post-synaptic activity creates a local eligibility trace. A publicly observed consequence prediction error modulates the local update; no global backpropagation or future outcome access. Explicitly specify the feature dictionary, weight bound, plasticity coefficient, eligibility decay, update order and nonlinearities **before** science.
2. **Metaplastic retention**: independently learned local importance reduces change to well-supported connections, with bounded homeostasis and an archived frozen prior context. If bank selection is used, it must depend on observed public evidence and carry its complete training/retrieval CPU/memory cost.
3. **Mandatory ablations**: same feature/capacity budget with metaplastic protection removed; same algorithm with three-factor modulation removed; same algorithm with bank archive disabled; stationary no-change sham; never-trained/zero model. This separates local Hebbian adaptation from generic bank storage.
4. **No oracle bank selection**: causal processing order must be observation -> pre-action forecast/action -> environment consequence -> score -> evidence update -> training. Feedback may influence *future* forecasts only. Reject duplicated, late, re-ordered, or private source data. Preserve complete cold-restorable model state.
