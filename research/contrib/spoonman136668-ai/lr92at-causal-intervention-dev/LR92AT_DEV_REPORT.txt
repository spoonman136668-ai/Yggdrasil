RESEARCH MECHANISM
------------------
Eight explicit three-input Boolean causal-program hypotheses (hypotheses are
specified in causal_programs.py.txt). An action is a fully specified binary
intervention do(x0,x1,x2): there are eight possible settings. After selecting
one setting, the learner sees one noisy binary consequence; it never sees
an evaluator's hidden true-program ID or a heldout query label during learning.
Bayesian updates persist eight float64 probabilities, 64 learned bytes,
101-byte SHA256-checked versioned snapshot. Source-locked candidate selects
interventions by minimizing exact two-step expected average Brier risk across
all eight possible intervention probes, adapting the next step after seeing
an outcome. It is fundamentally different from the failed archived/recent
prediction-bank gates; no phase/clock/regime, no transformers or tokens.
Static causal-program descriptions and planner compute ARE genuine resource
costs but are not counted in the 64 learned bytes.

FROZEN STUDY AND CUSTODY
------------------------
Protocol frozen_dev_protocol.json.txt and all four executable source/test
bytes committed at source-lock SHA above BEFORE scored fixture execution.
Two newly disjoint DEVELOPMENT seeds 76021921 and 76021922 (neither prior
exposed LR-92AP/AQ/AR/AS dev seeds nor protected original primary seeds).
Eight in-hypothesis programs plus two deliberately unfamiliar negative-control
programs, three independent replications per (seed, program) = 60 worlds:
48 known-hypothesis worlds + 12 unfamiliar-hypothesis worlds.
Each learner: eight training interventions per world = 480 public action/
consequence updates total, then eight heldout do-probe forecasts per world
= 480 nontraining probes, with no query feedback to the learner. All models
receive the same intervention budget, all possible actions and same fixed
noise draw indexed by public step within a world; chosen actions deliberately
differ across active-learning policies. Probe labels are an independent
fixed draw, shared across methods, and their analytic expectation also scored.
An 8-context independent tabular Beta learner gets a separate fully trained
posterior but no structural inductive bias. No cross-world reuse of training.

FROZEN COMPARISONS
------------------
1. CausalLookahead2, exact eight-program Bayesian posterior + 2-step planning.
2. ExactBayesIG, same eight-program posterior + optimal one-step entropy gain.
3. ExactBayesMyopicRisk, same posterior + one-step expected Brier minimization.
4. ExactBayesRandom, same posterior + independent non-oracle random do-actions.
5. TabularCoverage, independent Beta-Bernoulli fitted action-table with evenly
   distributed intervention budget (32 persistent bytes).
6. PermutedProgramSham, same Bayesian state and two-step planning but WRONG
   pre-frozen mapping from input interventions to causal program predictions.

Primary *development* endpoint: exact analytical expected Brier over all eight
heldout potential intervention action probes after eight public updates,
macro-averaged over 48 known-program worlds, stratified by the two dev seeds.
Also score 12 out-of-hypothesis worlds and whole-policy local wall time.
Development GO requires ALL: >=0.01 absolute gain vs the best of all four
conventional comparator arms IN EACH seed; OOD expected Brier <= best
conventional OOD +0.01; measured 60-world local policy wall-time <=2x
fastest conventional arm. These criteria and their source/seed hashes were
frozen before scoring. Their failure is DEV NO-GO; do not tune this cohort.
No SCIENTIFIC_PASS possible with this synthetic dev dataset.

DECISION / NEXT MECHANISM
--------------------------
DEVELOPMENT_NO_GO. This does NOT refute explicit causal programs as a useful
inductive bias: known-program expected Brier strongly beats tabular under
sparse interventions, and fixed permuted-program sham degrades. But a
conventional one-step Bayesian planner offers almost the same predictive
performance at far lower planning cost; the catalog breaks on unfamiliar
programs. No unique hypothesis-discovery, transfer or intelligence advantage.
STOP retuning this two-step planner on these now exposed 60 worlds. The next
falsifiable direction should test **building new causal hypotheses** after
unknown interventions rather than only choosing actions among a fixed known
catalog, compared with established structure-learning and active Bayesian
controls, with a NEW frozen disjoint source/budget/owner science boundary.
