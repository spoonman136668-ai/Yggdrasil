HYPOTHESIS & NON-ORACLE INTERVENTION CONTRACT
--------------------------------------------
The candidate keeps two induced bounded GF(2) polynomial hypothesis slots and uses disagreement between their predictions to choose x1 before observing s2, and x2 after observing public s2. It receives no regime identity, phase boundary, source truth table, future outcome, counterfactual evaluation label or teacher.
Each learner gets the same exogenous public s1 sequence, independent transition/terminal noise streams, equal opportunities to choose two binary actions per episode and one observed outcome.
Policies choose different interventions and therefore see different realized outcome contexts; matched action/information opportunity does NOT imply identical realized observations or compute.
All arms are scored prequentially on the SAME independent uniform 16-context evaluator-only expected-Brier panel before observing current outcomes. This removes the main selection bias from scoring only easier chosen actions.
Exact grammar-informed HMM control has all 256 candidate Boolean program structures but not the correct program, regime, future outcomes or switch schedule. Quartic A is out-of-class.
Fully trained conventional logistic regression replays chronological observed data in fixed periodic passes. Full-history, window-32 and decayed tabular Beta controls are also included.
