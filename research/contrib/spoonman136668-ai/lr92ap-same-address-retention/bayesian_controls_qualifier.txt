MODEL DESIGN AND PRECOMMITTED FIXTURE SCOPE
-------------------------------------------
1) SoftHMM14: two-regime fixed-hazard online HMM with symmetric transition
   hazard 1/32. Posterior is updated with observed Bernoulli likelihood; two
   regime emission banks receive posterior-weighted Q4 expected-count updates.
   Uses fixed 14 reachable (two-prior-action, current-action) bin patterns.
2) ApproxBayesChangePoint14: finite two-hypothesis filter with change hazard
   1/64, online soft-count updates, and changepoint alarm when old regime's
   posterior falls below 1/8; challenger copied to current on alarm. This
   is an explicit APPROXIMATION, NOT exact/untruncated Bayesian Online
   Changepoint Detection and NOT a fully fitted broad conventional control.

Each stores 2*448 outcome bytes + 16 prior-history-code bytes +
16 posterior bytes = 928 persistent learned/history bytes across 16 addresses,
7 active + 9 retained; no hidden phase labels, no future labels, no explicit
phase-clock/schedule. Snapshot is 964 bytes: version 4, state 928, SHA256 32.
Both emit forecasts before observing labels, with atomic invalid-step
rejection; 16-address isolation, deterministic replay, checkpoint parity,
corruption/wrong-format rejection, read-only forecasts and unannounced
switch indistinguishability are included in the eight local tests.

INTERPRETATION AND NEGATIVE EVIDENCE
------------------------------------
Soft HMM improves early A return relative to the prior hard-MAP comparator in
seed 73004303, but loses to it on B in BOTH fixtures and is slightly worse
on early A return in seed 73004304. Approx CPD loses much of its retained A
capability in seed 73004304 (Brier 0.4183), an important development negative.
None establishes superiority over the strongest matched conventional control.
There has been NO fixture hyperparameter search or post-primary adaptation.

Precision caveat: Q4-count posterior-weighted online updating is a bounded
approximate Bayesian filter, NOT exact posterior marginalization over model
parameter uncertainty and run lengths. Additional fully trained/fitted
Bayesian and regime-mixture baselines may be required by the original protocol.

IDENTIFIABILITY / SOURCE FENCES
-------------------------------
When A returns with no public change cue, its first forecast is identical to
the forecast of a world in which B continues after the same observed prefix;
first-step causal-regime identification is impossible without new information.

whereas these controls do not receive a schedule. Do not reinterpret cold
memory retrieval as spontaneous detection of an unseen return-to-A event.
