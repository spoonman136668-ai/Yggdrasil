OBSERVABLE CLOCK CONFOUND
------------------------
A learner able to count public observations (or infer the 64/64/64 schedule
over repeated worlds) can infer the evaluator's switch positions 64 and 128
without distinguishing causal hypotheses from observed interventions.
This creates a possible shortcut for a phase-aware predictor. A learner
that does not receive/retain the clock might not exploit the shortcut.
This is a PROTOCOL-VALIDITY RISK; it is not a measured native-model cheat,
not a scientific negative of the trained model, and not a proof that every
causal-memory comparison becomes worthless.

EXACT TWO-WORLD INDISTINGUISHABILITY CONTROL
--------------------------------------------
Construct two possible futures after the identical observed 128-step prefix
with the same current public action a=0. One latent world B continues:
p(Y=1)=0.85. In the other, A returns without an observable cue:
p(Y=1)=0.15. Both Bernoulli targets include independent 15% label noise.
A phase-blind model seeing exactly the same prefix and action must issue the
SAME forecast in both, regardless of architecture or intelligence.
Under equal prior over the worlds, Brier-optimal common forecast is 0.5,
expected score 0.2500. A hypothetical oracle with forbidden true-regime
knowledge has expected Brier 0.1275, difference +0.1225 at this single
first cold forecast. The bound is conditional on equal prior, this toy
action-consequence law, and no public switch cue. It is NOT a measured
0.1225 Brier improvement by any implemented model and does not limit
recovery after additional public observations.
At t=128 known schedule (64,64,64) says A, while equally valid hidden
schedule (64,128,64) says B; current public prefix alone cannot select one.
