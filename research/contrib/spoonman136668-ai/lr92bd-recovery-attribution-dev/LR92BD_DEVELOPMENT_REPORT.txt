QUESTION — FAILURE ATTRIBUTION, NOT NEW PERFORMANCE CLAIM
---------------------------------------------------------
Previously on LR-92BC, active interventions improved early-return Brier over
random actions but failed strong exact/conventional controls. This study
isolates whether failure was (1) missing/poor archived A causal hypotheses or
(2) selecting the wrong existing causal hypothesis under a hidden return.

The candidate, its update rules, its action selection, and all strong controls
are copied byte-for-byte from the preceding LR-92BC study. Only the source-
free world seeds/tag and a separately read-only diagnostic/evaluator change.
No model redesign, threshold tuning, donor, new controller, training restart,
accepted checkpoint or canonical native learner modification was attempted.

FROZEN DESIGN / INFORMATION CUSTODY
-----------------------------------
Two new disjoint synthetic development seeds: 96774011 and 96774012.
Two seeds x three families (cubic_rewrite, disjoint_cubic,
quartic_out_of_class) x four replicate worlds = 24 worlds and 5,263 public
two-stage episodes PER LEARNER. New smoke-only seed: 96774010.
Existing/exposed LR-92AQ through LR-92BC and protected LR-92AP seeds excluded.
Source-first Git freeze includes actual source SHA-256, development manifest,
predefined attribution thresholds and evaluator-only-oracle semantics; frozen
source files are unchanged after first scoring.

Nine arms: dual_active, dual_passive and dual_coverage (identical learning
state/update mechanism and different public intervention choices), exact
256-program grammar-informed Bayesian HMM control, full-history Beta,
window-32 Beta, decayed Beta, periodically trained full-history polynomial
logistic, and no-learning random. All observe one public action-consequence
episode per step, with identical public initial state and exogenous noise
streams but different chosen actions. COMPUTE AND REALIZED INPUTS ARE NOT
IDENTICAL; this is a source-free mechanistic DEVELOPMENT study, not a
resource-matched independent scientific acceptance.

Primary measured score: prequential EXPECTED terminal Brier over the SAME
uniform 16-context evaluator-only panel, evaluated before the current public
outcome. The learner receives only public s1, chosen x1, public s2, chosen x2
and terminal y. A/B/return times, true causal coefficients, outcome tables,
reference-panel counterfactual labels and oracle choice NEVER enter learner
prediction, action selection or update.

A **PRIVILEGED BEST-EXISTING-SLOT ORACLE** chooses at each step whichever of
the model's already learned (0–2) slots has the lowest evaluator-true
expected panel Brier. This is intentionally NOT implementable online:
it uses the hidden true regime and complete evaluator panel to provide a
*hindsight upper bound on performance* achievable by selecting the existing
archive correctly, without inventing new learned coefficients. It cannot
change action selection, training or memory. Its gain is not an intelligence
improvement or permitted scientific-control arm.

Frozen per-seed mechanistic categories:
- ARCHIVE_ACCURACY_LIMITED if privileged oracle return16 > best strong
  conventional/symbolic return16 +0.03;
- IDENTIFICATION_LIMITED if actual minus oracle >=0.03 and oracle <=
  best strong control return16 +0.01;
- otherwise MIXED_OR_INDETERMINATE.
Scientific PASS is NOT AVAILABLE for this diagnostic; no posthoc model choice
or rule adjustment can turn its result into a learning claim.

IMPORTANT CAUSAL INFERENCE LIMITATION
--------------------------------------
The privileged oracle explicitly knows what real learners CANNOT know
before seeing return observations. In particular, an unannounced return
is observationally ambiguous on its first step. The 0.080508 figure is a
hindsight lower bound on error for this memory, not an achieved score, a
feasible policy, proof of fast identifiable switching, an upper bound on
Bayesian capability, or general intelligence. The exact Bayesian model
beat the candidate substantially on B: 0.096251 vs 0.177730. The strongest
conventional early-return control beat the actual learner: 0.157152 vs
0.215813. The study does NOT justify deploying or integrating a model.
