COMPARISON DESIGN
-----------------
Two EXPOSED development seeds x three causal relationship families
(XOR, AND, random14) x four worlds = 24 independent fresh-world episodes.
Total 4,966 PUBLIC action/outcome observations per model (39,728 total
predict/update observations across eight learner arms).
Three private evaluator A/B/A dwell lengths between 48 and 88, different
in 24/24 worlds; change boundaries do not coincide with an episode reset.
All 14 temporal bins visited within EVERY world. Each learner starts
untrained independently in each world; no inter-world transfer/adaptation.
All learners receive same public episode start, current action and then
the same outcome only AFTER issuing the forecast. No latent phase,
relationship table, future consequence, or switch-time feature passed.
Identical source-locked parameters; no fixture hyperparameter search.
Every model's 16-address learned/history state <=928 serialized bytes.
Models' snapshot envelopes add 4 magic + 32 SHA256 checksum bytes.
A/HMM models are approximate bounded conventional controls; no claim
all possible conventional algorithms have been ruled out.
Reported Brier are MACRO means of 24 world-level prequential scores
(unweighted by variable dwell length); lower is better.
