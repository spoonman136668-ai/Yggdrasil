RESEARCH HYPOTHESIS / DIFFERENT MECHANISM
------------------------------------------
Construct novel causal algebraic relationships from independent public action-
consequence observations and preserve a compact witness basis for each public
task context. Store at most the rank-increasing GF(2) constraints (not full
predetermined truth tables, weights from an external model, or every event).
Test whether the learned A relationship is retained through acquisition of an
unrelated B relationship, restored cold and reusable on reachable interventions.
This is NOT global/local prediction gating, model promotion, training-owner
continuation or any new canonical learner. It uses the existing native-compatible
(s1, v, x1, s2, x2, y) two-stage PUBLIC schema, but is an independent source-free
mechanistic development cell, not code inserted into NativeEpisodeExperience.

SCOPE AND IMPORTANT LIMITS
--------------------------
- The public task-context bit v=0 occurs in A and A-return, while public v=1
  occurs in B. Therefore this study measures public-identity-separated
  retention/transfer, NOT detection of hidden unannounced phase switches.
- Learning conditions are noiseless deterministic Boolean GF(2) formulas with
  monomials of degree <=2. "Disjoint_parent" has unrelated output parent
  factors between public contexts, "quadratic_rewrite" requires constructed
  second-order interactions, and "cubic_unfamiliar" includes degree-3 laws
  intentionally out of representation scope. The candidate abstains with
  probability 0.5 when constraints are not identifiable or are contradicted.
- Model never receives hidden true coefficients, family choice, evaluator
  phase boundaries, seed labels, future labels, source actor results or oracle
  counterfactual labels. Family/seed metadata is evaluator-only.
- Synthetic source-free development is not original causal-world scientific
  first use under issue #426 and cannot establish intelligence/transfer on
  real-world data. Original 288 protected LR-92AP worlds remain unconsumed.
