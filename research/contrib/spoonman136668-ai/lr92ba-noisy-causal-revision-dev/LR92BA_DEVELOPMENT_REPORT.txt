HYPOTHESIS / RESEARCH DIFFERENCE
--------------------------------
The LR-92AZ compact deterministic GF(2) witness learner permanently invalidated
an entire causal context on encountering a contradictory public observation.
LR-92BA instead retains separate counts for both contradictory outcomes at
bounded public intervention addresses and continuously revises a degree<=2 GF(2)
coefficient vector by an explicit minimum-disagreement integer objective. No
prewritten complete causal programs or oracle coefficients are supplied. It
conservatively abstains when the best hypothesis cannot explain more than a
fixed, preregistered fraction of the observed public labels. A full exact
Bayesian posterior over all 2,048 terminal coefficient vectors (with same
public data and degree2 hypothesis family) supplies a stronger grammar-aware
control. This is a structural-constraint revision mechanism, not another
global/local prediction-bank selection or native model integration.
