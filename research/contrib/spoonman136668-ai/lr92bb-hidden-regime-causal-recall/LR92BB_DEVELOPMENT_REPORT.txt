QUESTION AND MECHANISM
----------------------
Do previously unseen cubic causal relationships survive an unannounced
A -> B -> A-return structural change when every phase reuses the SAME public
observation address? Can a bounded learned causal-structure archive infer the
new law from interventions and recall the old one without phase oracle or
preprogrammed complete causal functions?

Candidate: CausalRecall. Construct up-to-three-nonconstant-monomial GF(2)
programs plus optional constant using a 940-element symmetric sparse cubic
composition grammar; preserve two compact archives of public context/label
counts; fit minimum-disagreement causal polynomial structures; detect novel
contradictions through public error syndromes; retrieve prior structure by
likelihood of public outcomes. **Not native Yggdrasil integration** and not a
new owner/canonical trainer. The two-slot *structural archive* is distinct
from the previously rejected global/local prediction-bank gating experiments,
but its regime selector still requires scientific qualification.

NO PUBLIC SWITCH CUE: All A, B and A-return episodes use the SAME paired-view
address ((s1,0),(s1,1)), with varying public first action s1 and public x1,
s2, x2 and y. The model sees neither phase time/boundary, family label, true
program coefficients, world seed, future outcomes, teacher, nor evaluator-only
counterfactual predictions. World generator holds that information for scoring
and fully reproducible source-free synthetic episodes only.

24 independent new development worlds: seeds 94771101 and 94771102 x three
structural families (cubic_parent_rewrite, cubic_disjoint_parent,
quartic_unfamiliar) x four replicates. All seeds disjoint from earlier exposed
LR-92AQ through LR-92BA and protected LR-92AP original cohort. Random A/B/
A-return dwell, as specified in frozen manifest; 5,496 public episodes per arm.
Terminal labels have fixed independent 7% flip noise, public intermediate s2
25% flip noise. The unfamiliar quartic A relationship is outside the candidate
and exact cubic grammar. Its failure is expected to be honestly recorded.
