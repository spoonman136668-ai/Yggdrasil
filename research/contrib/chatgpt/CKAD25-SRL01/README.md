## Scope and information/action accounting

Each of 48 stationary hidden-hotspot synthetic cohorts sees **exactly three previous source-free worlds**, with two distinct publicly observed action/outcome pairs per world (six total historical outcomes). This is the existing native learner's historical slots 12–14 capacity; evidence remains grouped by world. An unseen persistent hotspot determines the next-world fault address with hidden probability 3/4, or one of three other addresses with 1/12 each. The candidate is **not told the hotspot, future outcomes or the 3/4 rate**; it jointly fits a 16-state posterior over four public hotspot addresses × hazard grid [1/4, 1/2, 3/4, 9/10]. Each target world contains exactly one fault.

All eight arms receive **the same six historical public outcomes per cohort**. They choose exactly two distinct action addresses based only on those six outcomes; each selected action receives a simulated source-free target consequence for detection scoring. **The 32 target worlds per seed are identical across arms and never admitted to training**. Four-site forecast Brier is scored from evaluator-only truth, pre-target. Additional 24 negative cohorts independently permute the hidden hotspot each world, so no stable role exists. Across 72 cohorts there are 432 training public witnessed outcomes and **4,608 simulated target probe consequences per arm**, not physical/native original-source actor calls.

Fully fitted matched-information controls are: native CKAD25 empirical per-address Beta expression; independent conventional Beta copy; independent full **16-state joint Bayes with exactly the SAME rate-grid/prior and actual six witness records**; inverse-propensity-free Dirichlet Laplace frequencies from positive observations; trailing-two-world Beta; fixed two; deterministic independently seeded random two. The native-like pure learner and independent joint Bayes must agree on every forecast/action, proving conventional equivalence, not uniqueness.
