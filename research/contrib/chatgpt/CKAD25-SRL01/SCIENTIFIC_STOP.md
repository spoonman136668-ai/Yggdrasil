## Why the strongest-control superiority gate is impossible for this exact candidate

For each three-world public witness history `E`, the candidate computes the normalized conventional joint posterior over **the same** 16 hypotheses (`hot_address ∈ four candidates × q ∈ {1/4,1/2,3/4,9/10}`) from the **same prior, likelihood and six witnessed outcomes** as an independently implemented, fully trained exact joint Bayes control. Both then compute identical predictive risk at each of four public addresses and select the same highest-risk two actions with the same tie-break. Source-only exhaustive correctness checks verify the resulting evidence, entire posterior, all four risk forecasts, and actions on **every possible** 3-world, 2-probe witness history in the supported class. This is a **model-class algebraic identity**, not just statistical similarity.

The original frozen **48 stationary synthetic cohorts, 1,536 target worlds**: empirical-Beta actions caught **1,157**, optimized shared-risk **1,202**, independent matched exact Bayes **1,202**; shared Brier **0.1420216951** vs empirical-Beta **0.1600303696**, *identical* to exact Bayes. Only **5** of the 48 paired cohorts showed strictly better detection, **3** worsened, **40** tied. The **24 disjoint no-stable-role negatives, 768 worlds** showed a candidate detection regression **387 vs Beta 396**, Brier regression **+0.0052933533**, still inside the frozen +0.020 tolerance but no unfamiliar-mapping gain. The matched strongest exact-Bayes tie holds there too.

Therefore **a native adapter run or more fitting optimization cannot produce a scientifically valid gain over that strongest control within this unchanged hypothesis class**. A positive original Beta comparison remains an engineering development signal; it is not a novel intelligence or generalization gain.
