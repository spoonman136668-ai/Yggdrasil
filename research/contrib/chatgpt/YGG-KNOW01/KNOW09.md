## Interpretative rules (nonnegotiable)

- A context with only one action has **no empirical within-context action contrast**. Both actions observed is *necessary for overlap* but NOT sufficient for causal identification, because randomization/exchangeability and hidden confounding are not independently established. Repeated mixed outcomes are **not proof of contradiction** or new latent causal structure.
- Retrospective post-training forecasts on consumed historical witnesses are **in-sample**, not cold unfamiliar-world transfer or prospective learning gains.
- The 21,343,681 historical NKR Q/P/Q rows do **not** become action-consequence observations by themselves. The source-free four-fact candidate overlay and synthetic fact-to-two-bit views are deliberately excluded from this audit.
