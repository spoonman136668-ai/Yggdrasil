KNOW39 separated fast regime belief and persistent storage and improved Brier against KNOW38 on *matched synthetic worlds*, yet failed a fully fitted HMM and no native gain was verified. This next mechanism assessment **does not retune KNOW39**. It tests the exact unchanged KNOW39 source on *new graph structures* and asks whether its beliefs select good actions under interventions. FIT graphs are linear Boolean F1/F2; HELD graphs are different nonlinear H1/H2 with four of eight contexts having genuine action effects. Hidden outcomes are sampled only for the **shared, forced intervention**. The evaluator scores each model's pre-outcome shadow choice of a0 or a1 using evaluator-only counterfactual expected value. All learners see identical (t,x,y) and are denied graph/seed/phase/oracle. An unexecuted policy proposal is **not** active-intervention learning; an actual chosen-action feedback experiment needs separate authorization and evidence.
