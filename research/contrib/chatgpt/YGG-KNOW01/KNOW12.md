**Independent contributor:** Yggdrasil KNOW research. **Canonical owner:** Unified Causal Intelligence Amalgam Work / PR #1278. **Base:** research PR #1327 at `7616e748a332b5983e2973d55385ed77243507f8`. ## Frozen before implementation/scoring

The pre-registration `know12_protocol.json` was committed as Git parent **`0dfbd367d54cebcc4155683eda57542b48109c89`** *before implementation code, focused tests and any synthetic primary result*. The original project source is pinned at Amalgam `86264b41a08d49f558418c0fba367f3a2636befa`. New developmental seeds are fixed at **404/505/606**, with 2 tasks per seed, 64 source entities per task, 32 fitted and 32 entity-disjoint held. 4 **unordered candidate Q/P/Q predicates** (P930–P933) encode binary observations, only one of which is causally predictive, while three independent distractors are balanced 8/8 in each fitted truth-mode stratum. A simulator-private role varies across seed/task but is **not passed to the learner or trained controls**.

**Question:** Can a fixed-footprint, tokenless native causal component *identify the predictive factual attribute from explicit action→consequence witnesses* rather than being handed the relationship key, and retain that acquired readout when a second task is learned? Unlike KNOW11, which directly supplies P910/P911 and an explicit mode mapping, the candidate must examine **all four** attributes using only source-typed direct facts and separately witnessed outcomes. No factual triple is relabelled as an outcome.

## Mechanism and negative controls

The existing `NativeContextBasis(context_bits=1, max_degree=1)` is fitted from **both actions of 32 FIT entities** for every candidate feature. A feature is eligible only if all public action–outcome rows are supported and GF(2) source-consistent; **exactly one eligible feature** must exist or the selector abstains. The selected feature's sole numeric bit is passed to the native basis. The candidate retains two separate address slots for A/B and is cold-restored; the original Amalgam checkpoint, four trained heads, archived historical image and protected source lease are unchanged.

The **independently implemented exhaustive conditional symbolic candidate-property tournament** and the **fully fitted depth-two tree feature tournament** both see the same public candidate attributes, fit outcomes, task/phase addresses, and action opportunities. Action-only and facts-only are weaker controls. Source-free fixture negatives enforce **tie/duplicate selectors abstain**, missing or conflicting facts abstain, incorrect provenance abstains, balanced source generation and no hidden role sent to fitting. The A→B→A return equality is explicitly **separate-slot isolation**, *not learned interference repair or same-address retention*.

## Gate interpretation

- **Engineering PASS** requires all 6 role selections, complete four-input FIT support, complete 384 held predictions, isolated native fit+restore, unchanged historical and original Amalgam checkpoint, and source-specific Linux/Windows full-final regression and historic-source compatibility.
- **Scientific superiority gate:** native mean held Brier at least **0.01 better** than *both* equally informed fully fitted symbolic and tree controls, while retaining old readouts, on independently admissible untouched source. If the native/symbolic/tree all score **0** on these deterministic synthetic laws, classify **`DEV_NO_GO_STRONGEST_CONTROL_TIE`** even though feature selection and engineering learning succeeded.
- **Information caveat:** simulator creates synthetic sources under an experimental four-feature class. The outcome rule is generated by the evaluator, not discovered by the official Yggdrasil model. A unique selector is an observed-development identifiability result within this narrow design, not general causal discovery, real-world semantics, action-value gain against exact control, or canonical learning.
- **Stop:** One pre-frozen source-safe trial. Preserve any null result without retuning role index, seeds, outcomes, thresholds, controls, action counts or held entity sets. The authenticated model owner and source owner retain exclusive rights to checkpoint promotion and first-use (#426). No trainer/scheduler/controller, acceptance or deployment.
