**Change:** Original `NativeContextAccumulator(context_bits=4)` now sees **four direct, source-typed binary Q/P/Q factual observations** (0–15) plus a public proposed binary action. No source-derived balanced-partition, entropy, expected gain, private hidden role or inferred Q/P/Q path is supplied as native input. Two native updates at **the same address 0**, with `max_degree=4` (degree 3 falsified as unable to express the raw-feature/action interaction). Cold restoration and complete 32 historical query readouts are checked before/after the second update.

**How utility is labeled:** This synthetic lab can instantiate four separate counterfactual causal worlds with **the same 34-entity factual source graph** per seed. Each of 16 probe raw patterns and each action is executed independently against each of the four hidden-role worlds; the four binary witnessed consequences are disclosed. A utility label is **1 only if exactly two outcomes are zero and two are one**, else 0. This is an expensive, world-family-specific *cross-world rehearsal*: 16 patterns × 2 actions × 4 role worlds × 2 TRAIN seeds = **256 witnessed synthetic training actions**, 64 source-pattern/action utility rows. No held-world action consequences or roles are used for training. This is NOT normal single-world online active learning; the four-role hypothesis structure and post-action candidate-consistency filtering remain supplied analytically.

**Held:** Three fresh source-identical seed graphs (each 34 entities, opaque role-independent IDs, all 16 raw feature patterns in probe and held), × four hidden roles = **12 synthetic worlds**. Each active policy can issue two witnessed interventions per world, with equal information and action budget. Native selects the query with highest **learned raw-feature utility forecast**, and after observing its consequence the same existing exact role-consistency filter updates candidates. Critically, the learned scorer was trained only with the *full four-role candidate set*. If it cannot adapt its second query to the reduced posterior, record engineering target **FAIL** rather than substituting an analytic second-step selector.

**Strong controls** see the same 256 training action consequences and public feature/action inputs: exact symbolic utility table, fully fitted depth-five conventional decision tree, original fully refitted native basis; analytic *candidate-aware* oracle provides a stronger upper bound. Random/passive/zero controls share the query budget and source. Causal identification accuracy and actions, source/owner custody, full-process cost and old→new→old cold retention are recorded. The full original-world first-use issue #426 is not touched, and the historical 21,343,681-row TRAIN and Amalgam joint SHA remain pinned, read-only.

**Frozen engineering criterion:** native identifies 12/12 held hidden roles within two actions and retains old 32 queries exactly, with verified cold restore and full dual-OS original+KNOW+G29 regression and real archive check. Report any failure without post hoc adjustment. **Scientific superiority criterion** requires improvement over *every* fully trained equally informed exact symbolic, conventional and native-refit control on a newly authorized original-world cohort; tie is `DEV_NO_GO_STRONGEST_CONTROL_TIE`. A finite raw-feature truth-table learned from exhaustive synthetic cross-world rehearsal does not establish novel world representation, native autonomous experiment design, or open-world generalization.
