## Problem and hypothesis

Gate24 showed a trainable symmetric interaction can fit artificial XOR but, when unconditionally active on the old OR law, gets an old-law wrong. Gate25 asks whether **only public action-consequence feedback** can switch between two *already exposed and fitted* shadow predictors without destroying either historical expert. This explicitly does NOT test acquisition of an unknown model or intelligence generalization. The same public `((1,a),(1,b))` observations appear under both incompatible laws; with **no public regime bit**, no possible policy can know the switch time before observing a distinguishing public outcome. Early switch mistakes must be counted, not erased. The old model must remain bit-exact.
