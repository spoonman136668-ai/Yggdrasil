## 1. Question and falsifiable mechanism

Can a bounded, tokenless, non-transformer learner discover predictive equivalence classes *from public interventions*, then reuse their learned action-consequence relations on held-out causal topologies with less predictive error and fewer costly interventions than strong equally informed controls? No true state labels, generator graph, oracle segmentation, latent cardinality, source code, mechanism grammar, or model parameters reach the candidate.

Proposed cell dynamics (mechanism, not yet implemented):
- Observe only public `(observation, action, next_observation, optional public reward)` streams; perform each prediction before receiving the outcome.
- Construct provisional local predictive states from public histories; **split** a state only after repeated, statistically justified disagreement in action-conditioned consequence distributions; **merge** states only after sufficient action-conditional predictive equivalence. Preserve counterexamples and uncertainty. Reject a split that produces no predictive benefit after complexity charge.
- Learn sparse directed relations between state contracts from local transition witnesses. Neighbor messages may carry *public-evidence summaries only*, not generator truth or a global correct-answer oracle. An ablation must prove whether these relations contribute beyond suffix tables.
- Hard ceilings: 128 committed predictive states, 256 directed relations, maximum history window 8 observed action/observation pairs, maximum 16 active provisional hypotheses per committed state, deterministic eviction and admission order. Never allocate more resources or acquire new permissions autonomously.
- Learning is online and bounded; retain a checksum-validated snapshot with cold prediction and policy equivalence. State *function*, not byte count alone, must survive A→B→A interference.

The concrete split/merge statistical rule, constants, representation and checkpoint format must be independently frozen in an implementation-specific amendment **before any calibration data are viewed**. If that is not possible, CALIBRATION DOES NOT START. Do not infer success from a selected action-conditioned feature or a larger suffix table.

## 3. Comparator set; fair information and compute

Fully fit all applicable controls before looking at calibration evaluation scores: action-conditioned HMM/POMDP with learned emissions/transitions and multiple TRAIN-only restarts, predictive-state representation (PSR) with learned action-observation operators, a full-context/tabular suffix predictor, a compact recurrent state estimator trained exclusively on public histories, and an active Bayesian intervention-selection policy using public evidence. A constant/no-action baseline is a negative reference, *not* a competitive comparator.

Each predictor sees identical permitted public TRAIN events and identical evaluation prefixes. Predictive Brier is compared first under **common externally randomized actions**, to prevent a better policy being misrepresented as a better model. Separately test active decisions with identical total action and observation budgets, priced probes and matched terminal-reward/accuracy targets. Controls may use their justified strong capacity; also report matched-parameter, matched-memory and matched-compute variants, training wall time, inference latency, effective bytes and model evaluations. Never substitute a deliberately weakened HMM as the strongest comparator. Fully trained here means convergence diagnostics, fixed training-only hyperparameter selection and restarts, no generator-parameter initialization or eval tuning.

- Its mean heldout Brier must be at least `0.020` (lower-is-better, minimum theoretical gain to perfection `>=0.020`). Primary candidate superiority threshold is provisionally `0.005` absolute Brier (candidate lower). This condition prevents the CCE-01/03 mathematically unreachable thresholds from recurring; it does **not** by itself establish that `0.005` is empirically achievable.

Demonstrate by an evaluator-only intervention contrast that the environment is distinguishable with the allotted actions.
- Confirm the strongest fully fitted controls have nontrivial accuracy and calibrated probabilities, stable train-only convergence, and resource use that fits the declared research budget. Explicitly record the **cost/accuracy Pareto frontier** for the active Bayesian comparator. Do not declare a cost-saving target feasible solely from random-policy performance.
