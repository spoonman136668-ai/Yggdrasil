## New falsifiable hypothesis
A scalar equilibrium energy / detailed-balance reversible transition model cannot express nonzero cycle current. A learned, bounded *antisymmetric transition flux* can discover the sign of a previously unknown action-dependent directed-cycle relationship from action/consequence witnesses. This is a nonconservative dynamical mechanism, **not** renaming a conventional Bayesian posterior as energy.

Exact structural diagnostic: for a four-node ring with clockwise probability q and counterclockwise probability (1-q) per edge, cycle affinity A=4 log[q/(1-q)]. Detailed balance forces A=0, whereas q=.8 gives A=4 log 4; q=.2 reverses it. This proves a representation boundary only, not an intelligence advantage.

## Immutable source-free synthetic cohort
6 independent primary seeds: 721101, 721127, 721147, 721169, 721181, 721199. Three scenario families per seed:
- DRIFT: u=0 has clockwise probability .8, u=1 has .2.
- REVERSIBLE: both actions have .5.
- ACTION_NULL: both actions have .8 (action does not change the relationship).
For each world/action, reveal exactly 8 training transitions with observed public (state, action, next_state). No hidden rule, direction value or scenario label supplied to learners. Held: 256 fresh transitions per state/action, independently sampled after fit, immutable and never trained on. Same observed training witnesses and held outcomes for all arms.

## Arms and required comparisons

All arms see equal transitions; D is an essential control, not a weak baseline. Report scoring Brier, policy direction accuracy, negative action-difference alarms, per-arm prediction calls and timing on the same host. Do not call B a novel scientific gain if it ties D.

## Frozen decisions
1. Mathematical boundary: conservative cycle-affinity identity and exact directed-cycle counterexample must hold.
2. Developmental representation feasibility: in DRIFT, B improves Brier over A by >=.02 and chooses the correct clockwise-favoring intervention in >=5/6 worlds.
3. Negative controls: no false action-sign declaration when the two actions share the same law in REVERSIBLE and ACTION_NULL, using a fixed confidence rule (posterior means differ by >=.30); retaining action-0 exact readout after fitting action-1, serialized cold restore 18/18.
4. **Scientific superiority** requires the candidate to beat the best fully fitted equally informed conventional/symbolic control on held Brier by >=.01 **and** preserve negative controls, or demonstrate >=2x measured whole-process improvement with held Brier at most .005 worse. Structural representation feasibility or a weak-baseline improvement does not suffice.
5. Do not replay consumed primary seeds, tune thresholds on their results, or reinterpret an engineering/source replay as original-world scientific acceptance. Keep any failure.
6. Exact-source full native dual-platform regression/atlas and independent scientific original first-use remain separate gates. Even a development positive is not integration authority.
