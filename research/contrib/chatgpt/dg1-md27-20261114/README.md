This external Yggdrasil research is a genuinely different dynamical environment from the blocked-door MiniGrid sequence. It **does not** change native Yggdrasil weights, research owners, controllers, accepted refs, main, or the autonomous loop.

## Frozen hypothesis and task

Preregistration commit `148e20fedcea143344a9c596dd84d7ebe8fa6c93` came before the first implementation or scientific result. The original standalone stdlib `CausalRelay-v1` contains two public wiring bits, a public desired target sign, and an *independent hidden polarity*. The agent can make an optional physically charged probe (cost 0.08) and then an irreversible A/B intervention (cost 0.03, +1 terminal goal or -0.4 failure). Without the paid probe, two hidden laws produce exactly the **same public initial observation** and require opposite actions. The unit negative control proves this is information-identifiable only after intervention. Policies never see simulator private polarity.

Exactly 240 shared teacher episodes (`1020001..1020240`) physically probe, then apply alternating A/B without hidden oracle. Each public context (00,10,11) receives 80 complete paid action-effect observations. The withheld 01 context occurs **only** in distinct toy heldout seeds `121..144`; no train episode ever shows that combination. The causal composition candidate learns relative action-effect signs for three contexts and composes the fourth with its explicitly stated factorization prior. A **fully trained conventional factorized maximum-likelihood model** is the strongest matched comparator. A fully trained joint lookup, a fully trained linear logistic model, passive no-probe, fixed probe and scripted known-physics ceiling are also evaluated from the same teacher data.

## Frozen disjoint toy result

The actual source-bound [dual-platform pre-first-use CI run 43587477914](https://github.com/spoonman136668-ai/Yggdrasil/actions/runs/43587477914) completed successfully: six original MD27 unit/negative tests on Ubuntu and Windows, and strict Ubuntu/Windows non-timing equality across all 7 arms’ paid physical actions, observable causal consequences, checkpoints and rewards. **All 10 frozen toy gates passed.**

The candidate gained +1.4 utility over trained lookup and +0.50333333 over no-probe; cold checkpoint reloading reproduced all frozen action traces exactly. **It gained exactly 0.0 over the fully trained factorized conventional baseline.** This proves a task-level need for physical causal information and an appropriate compositional structural prior, **not a novel or superior intelligence-learning algorithm**.
