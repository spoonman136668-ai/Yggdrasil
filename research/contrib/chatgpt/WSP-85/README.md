# WSP85 — Interventions that distinguish competing causal hypotheses

**Status: developer-only prospective experiment.** WSP84's frozen
14/15 NEGATIVE, including 1344 abstentions in seed10011 and poor zero-shot
transfer, remains unchanged. The original registered WSP80 SELFTEST
seeds 5601–5616 and independent source-first-use authority are not
available to this new experiment.

## Why this experiment matters

The WSP84 learner assembled a numerical three-input Boolean operator from
paid observations, but several equally simple, observation-consistent
causal explanations disagreed on untested histories. Instead of
allowing the learner to invent labels, WSP85 tests whether it can
**choose physically paid interventions that differentiate them**.

The new source keeps the broad WSP84 synthetic six-bit causal family
but uses **six NEW unregistered worlds** 10201–10206 (four hidden
operator worlds, two memoryless controls), preserving independent
SHA256-secret physics parameters. The source is not imported by the
action policy, numeric/symbolic learners, or neural controls.

For each world, the three arms independently pay one continuous,
no-reset 384-action session:
1. \`hypothesis_active\`: first 144 paid StageA actions shared with
   controls, then 240 feedback-chosen interventions.
2. \`hash_random\`: independently paid identical StageA, then 240
   source-independent hash-random actions.
3. \`cyclic_passive\`: independently paid identical StageA, then 240
   fixed rotating actions.

Each intervention selector periodically fits an 8,000-member public
three-slot balanced-mask Boolean hypothesis family using only the
charged public trajectory, keeping up to 512 consistent hypotheses
for bounded choice. Unknown truth-table cells remain unknown rather
than being labeled from the source. It chooses the action with maximal
model disagreement, a two-step lookahead and unseen public
action-triple bonus. No source-private target, mask, oracle or free
trial is available to selection; it must pay for each consequence.

**All arms fit exactly the SAME original WSP84 numerical weight
compiler, independent equally expressive symbolic estimator, original
WSP83 old two-XOR negative, original genuine WSP84 25→64 ReLU→6
three-restart Adam (fresh restart seed per world/arm), exact paid
memorizer and memoryless fixed control.** A promising active policy
must beat BOTH random and passive *on heldout predictions*, not just
change action distribution. Frozen rules include at least +500
confident correct over random, +1000 over passive across the four
novel worlds, >=3 novel worlds with >=16,000/16,512 confident correct,
zero confident wrong and fewer retained causal alternatives. A
negative result must remain NEGATIVE.
