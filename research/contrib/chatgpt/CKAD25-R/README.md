This is a deliberately **retrospective, consumed-fixture diagnostic**, not a new Yggdrasil scientific first-use, not a new learner, and not a re-score to promote the original CKAD25 evidence.

**Why:** Native CKAD25 real cumulative training persisted and cold-restored successfully but all four previously withheld 3-bit synthetic examples were abstentions. **Interpretation:** Even 4/4 recovered predictions would only show a conventional sparse prior can address the specific underdetermined synthetic fixture. It would not establish unknown-family transfer, independent generalization, superiority over the conventional prior or the original native model's scientific success. A negative remains negative. No re-use of old held worlds as fresh validation. Model source and checkpoint immutable.
