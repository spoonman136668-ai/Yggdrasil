## Scientific question

Can a learner with numerical state registers **acquire a coordinate system** from real action transitions and **generate a previously unnamed composition of operator primitives** from further experience, with no supplied family label or latent encoder?

The numeric experience-order coordinate map is inherited from WSP50: full source action-0 cycle, no oracle. This is **not arbitrary representation invention**: cycle certification and source-action choice are still programmed. The novelty under test is generic rule-program synthesis within a bounded primitive grammar: INC/DEC, one-bit rotates, individual bit XORs; compositions of at most three operations. The hypothesis space is generated by software, frozen by grammar, not independently invented. A candidate rule survives only if it agrees with actual observed action-1 replies. Unseen predictions require unanimous predictions among surviving programs; otherwise the candidate abstains. Exact observed facts always override rules.

## Frozen source discipline

- Six hidden families: three target rule compositions, quadratic and random negatives, and invalid-cycle negative; widths 8, 6, 4; three interchangeable observable label encodings; disjoint DEV/PRIMARY seeds.
- One real current-state trajectory per encoded condition. The learner is never passed a world family, latent state, decoder, teacher function, correct program, output label beyond observations, or ability to query arbitrary states.
- First N actual action-0 transitions certify and register experience-order coordinates. Up to 12 action-1 interventions chosen from candidate disagreement, navigated by real action-0 transitions, all actions charged; no free reset.
- Compare frozen WSP47 same-trajectory GF2 model, exact one-hot factual memory, a stronger conventional affine consistency control, independent exhaustive batch grammar identification and erased memory. A randomized action-selection comparison remains a separately stipulated control not yet claimed.
- Explicitly retain all incorrect confident predictions, underdetermined cases, invalid-cycle outcomes, source counts, memory payload, fit/search time, cold restore and program version-space after each phase.

## Preregistered scientific thresholds

Across all held-out positive conditions, at least 95% exact unseen action-1 answers, zero incorrect confident answers and at least +20 percentage-point gain over the strongest same-evidence frozen control on every condition. On quadratic and random negatives, at least 90% abstention on unobserved action-1 queries after 12 probes with no incorrect confident answers. Every observed fact must be recalled; invalid cycle must not grant a coordinate model. A miss is MIXED, not retuned. Source/parity/cold-fact failures invalidate. No independent SCIENTIFIC_PASS, general intelligence, complete training replacement or neural-training superiority claim.
