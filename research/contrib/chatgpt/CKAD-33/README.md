# CKAD33 — Native numeric causal representation compilation

**Research-only, not accepted native or scientific promotion.** Freeze/preregistered before first source use at commit `dce7ad6e81d33d5cf69731440397b86a37928fc0` on isolated parent `de90aa1edb60f3ea4d07843f4d2c5e93ab440c0c`.

## Why this gate follows CKAD32

CKAD32's causal-hypothesis tournament often reduced its *conventional supplied* 2,880-law posterior to a unique rule, while native prediction remained unknown on held inputs. CKAD33 tests whether the **unchanged, already-present native numeric polynomial checkpoint substrate** can write source-witnessed causal constraints as coefficients and support, persist them across six unrelated learned slots, and make cold predictions on unseen actions.

This is not new architecture or unrestricted causal discovery. The candidate is supplied the degree-2 class in advance. A native checkpoint achieving the same accuracy as a conventional GF(2) solver is an engineering feasibility result, **not SCIENTIFIC_PASS**. Exact Bayes is the strongest in-class full-data control.

## Frozen experiment

- Use a fresh deterministic, input-only rank-greedy selection of **16 public actions** giving full degree-2 feature rank, four independent public calibration interventions and 12 completely unseen held inputs.
- In-class: three independent two-interaction Boolean causal rules. OOD: three independent cubic-perturbed rules **not** representable by degree-2 polynomials.
- Candidate fits `NativeContextAccumulator.stage_update(max_degree=2)` on only 16 observed outcomes per target, saves numeric source support/weights, then uses four further witnessed outcomes as a fail-closed *admission gate*; calibration outcomes never refit candidate coefficients.
- Strong controls: unmodified `NativeCumulativeLearner` with all 20 source observations, its existing sparse repair, independent fitted GF(2) on 16, exact 2880-rule Bayesian posterior on 20, fitted conventional logistic on 20, frequency, and sham. OOD also has a fully fitted 20-witness conventional degree-3 GF(2) comparator and uncalibrated compiled-native ablation.
- Add six independently witnessed 16-row pressure slots. Verify all 32 previous native readouts unchanged, byte-exact cold checkpoint, provenance, and atomic contradiction rejection.
- Exact source budget per seed: **216** public source actions (60 in-class, 60 cubic negative and 96 pressure), **72** scored held composite cases spread across two families, never source-fed. Pilot seeds 13531 and 13532 only. Primary seeds 23531, 23532, 23533 remain untouched.
- Frozen engineering feasibility threshold: both pilots produce in-class Brier ≤0.01, at least 0.10 lower than unchanged native, ≥95% native held primitive coverage, identical in-class Brier to independent GF(2), at most naive out-of-class Brier, **zero confidently wrong OOD accepted bits** and reject ≥2/3 cubic slots at calibration. Source/memory and all controls must PASS. Otherwise `SCREEN_NO_GO` or `INVALID`.
- No automatically authorized primary even if engineering is feasible: conventional parity is expected, and a new independent source family/mechanism would be needed to test superiority over conventional.

Persistent numeric weights do not prove native understanding, general intelligence, transfer across unknown classes or autonomy.
