# WSP-126 — Incremental latent causal-factor rank growth

**Source-safe development; original scientific first-use DENIED. No accepted native model merge/deployment.**

Previous WSP-125 generated 128 new rank-one relationship masks and achieved
perfect new-identity relabel transfer, but **optimized symbolic inference
tied exactly** and its off-family negative failed 25%. Earlier WSP-113,
AAI-12S, DG1-SL09 and FKP-21 consumed or falsified overlapping bounded
grammar ideas; none is reopened as a fresh independent result.

This successor deliberately changes the **representation capacity** rather
than merely increasing the number of examples. The model starts without
any learned latent interaction feature. Each true observed 16-bit
action-effect row that is linearly independent of earlier rows allocates
an additional persistent signed-int32 basis coefficient, up to four.
The complete effect law is a hidden 16×16 bit matrix of exactly GF(2)
rank 4, generated by XOR of four independent hidden outer products.
The source's correct coefficients, factor supports and relationship
matrix are not provided to either learner. However, **GF(2) arithmetic
and rank≤4 are SUPPLIED mathematical assumptions**, not newly invented
computational primitives. A four-factor structural addition is not proof
of general relationship-type invention or intelligence.

`6a2ec796c85d66a2a6b6eb4ec0e3ab09990c49d1`.
Source fixture is inspectable and synthetic, not a trusted blinded oracle.

## Large-first experimental shape

- 128 hidden independent **rank-4 causal laws**, 16×16 public typed
  interventions per law, 160 unique paid source calls/law/**arm** =
  **20,480 charged training effects per arm**.
- First eight typed-left rows are completely probed (128 effects).
  Their observed independent rows select a numerical basis and pivot
  columns. The final eight rows receive exactly four pivot-column
  probes each (32 effects). The remaining **96 unqueried pairs/law**
  are held (12,288 total fit held per arm).
- If the initial eight rows fail to reveal all four independent
  directions, **do not extrapolate** into later unqueried relationships:
  an unseen factor could otherwise produce confident errors.
  The exact 160-call policy, cohort and thresholds are frozen. A
  DEVELOPMENT_NO_GO on low information coverage is accepted as a
  substantive identifiability limitation.
- Two independently seeded new opaque-identity views/law =
  **256 transfer views ×256 relations =65,536 decisions/arm** at zero
  further paid actions. These are still only **128 underlying source
  laws**, NOT 256 newly discovered law structures.
- Source-only negatives on 64 laws add one independent rank-one effect
  so that the new law has **rank 5** and is OUTSIDE the supplied
  model family. All **16,384** predictions are scored with correct,
  confident error and abstention counts. No post-negative tuning.
- Full paid-source transcript parity for numerical and independently
  implemented optimized GF(2) symbolic row-space solver. The latter
  is a mathematically capable, equal-information control. Their
  parity means no novel advantage of numerical weight storage.
- Other baselines: rank-one-only ablation, opaque ID memo, no-update.
  A passive-symbolic control receives the same number of random paid
  source actions, but exact low-rank completion from arbitrary sparse
  entry patterns is **not qualified here**; its inference conservatively
  abstains. It is NOT a fair active-policy superiority reference.
- Fully trained matched conventional neural learner, independent
  original first-use, full-process resource parity and repeated cost
  qualification are OUTSTANDING. Neither total time, RAM, nor learning
  speed superiority is claimed.

Persistent numerical basis: at most four learned 16-bit row-vector
coefficients per law plus sixteen per-row coefficient masks (20
signed-int32 slots maximum = 80 bytes per law **excluding** the full
observed evidence, Python objects, snapshots, source and scoring).
The model grows one actual allocated factor per independent
observation, reconstructs missing row consequences only after
rank-four identifiability, and replays evidence on cold restoration.
A digest-repaired forged checkpoint coefficient must fail.
