## Method

Each development world has 16 four-bit public contexts mapped to a new permutation of opaque subject identifiers, 12 legal subjects and four held subjects. Only engineering seeds `122900..122903` are allowed; 12 source calls are used on the initial world and four on each subsequent world, 24 total.

The `NUMERIC_GROW` method begins with only affine/degree-one numeric coefficients. It programs signed-int32 polynomial coefficient slots by solving verified consequence constraints without gradient descent. A witnessed contradiction of the affine family triggers bounded allocation of higher-order conjunction terms. Its output is withheld if the verified observations do not uniquely determine it. A source-blind query selector prioritizes uncertain public contexts; source outcomes cannot influence selection until after querying.

Every new observation is retained in a content-addressed evidence ledger, duplicate or contradictory evidence is rejected, and a cold snapshot is recomputed against its ledger before restoration. A digest checks consistency, **not trustworthiness of the source**.

Equal-information controls include: `NUMERIC_AFFINE_FIXED` (no structural growth), `SYMBOLIC_TABLE_STRONG` (constant-time exact contextual memo), `NO_UPDATE`, and an additional independently coded `SYMBOLIC_ANF_STRONG` (same polynomial prior as numeric, opposite-pivot GF(2) solver). The symbolic ANF comparator was added as a *post-development-fixture falsification check*, **not** as a change to frozen original scientific outcomes or an unearned numeric victory.
