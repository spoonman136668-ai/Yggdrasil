## New hypothesis
Two otherwise independently selected numerical neural feature banks can sometimes disagree about a pseudo-memory even when the fast teacher is confident. Rejecting teacher-generated labels unless *both learned neural teachers confidently agree*, and unless **already-paid** Phase-B real observations contradict them, may improve pseudo-label quality and unfamiliar-world reliability. This hypothesis can fail: the same Phase-A evidence and narrow preprogrammed feature grammar may make both teachers confidently wrong in the same way.

## Strong controls and information accounting
- **Primary matched-k control**: sample **k** proposals with a fixed independent SHA-seeded RNG from the **exact same primary teacher's confident proposal pool**, *without* secondary-teacher agreement or Phase-B contradiction filtering. Thus same primary source data, pseudo-label generator, training budget, hidden/readout architecture, synthetic-update count and (accounted) teacher/committee inference CPU; the **selection information** differs. Never describe it as equal quality of information.
- Real Phase-A memory replay: k genuine older charged observations retained externally, **stronger raw-memory information control**, same Phase-B and updates. Matched-B-repeat: k actual B observations randomly repeated, equal count but no past A knowledge. B-only lower-compute diagnostic and deliberately action-scrambled teacher-dream negative.

## Scientific limits
- The two teachers share physical experience and the same preprogrammed action-gated signed-conjunction architecture, so agreement is **not** a calibrated proof of truth. Phase-B charged facts are already available to all models. A favorable result could come from stronger selection information rather than a superior memory write algorithm.
- Only readout weights are updated in the durable student; the learned hidden bank is inherited and frozen. This is an experiment on selective *direct numerical weight consolidation*, **not** a universally novel neural feature grammar, language, open-world learning, self-generated factual discovery or general intelligence.
- This SELFTEST is not independent `SCIENTIFIC_PASS`, authorized DEV/PRIMARY first-use, deployment, or official FORGE/Yggdrasil autonomous-loop integration.
