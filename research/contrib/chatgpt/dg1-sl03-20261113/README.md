## What it tries
Native HC09 two-bank causal-rule learning is supplemented with offline replay of **real paid physical observations only**. At fixed eight-step rest boundaries, the candidate searches contiguous latent 1, AB and ABA causal contexts by fitting 40-rule version-space masks. It changes the predictor only when retrospective physical-event scoring favors the inferred partition over the unchanged online learner. No hidden phase or evaluator truth is passed to any learner. Imagined predictions are diagnostic only and cannot become training experience.

The six arms are: native no-sleep, independent conventional online, selective sleep, independently coded conventional selective sleep, sham sleep, and same-compute online consolidation. Every arm shares the same paid action stream in 24 prospective worlds. Equal physical events do not prove equal whole-process compute. **A conventional tie or online same-compute tie blocks a claim of a novel sleep-specific Yggdrasil advantage.**
