## The specific advance

For a declared 6-bit intervention, four bits represent a 16-value native context, one is the action and one is a nuisance factor. A local (context, action) coordinate is **certified** only when that exact intervention was paid with nuisance=0 **and** nuisance=1, and the observed effects agree. If just one is available, it is NOT installed as an invariant native fact. If both disagree, the entire proposed update is rejected atomically.

At the fixed **32-effect** budget, a chosen four-intervention quartet (two actions × two nuisance values) certifies one complete native action choice. Eight such quartets yield **eight locally supported choices**, leaving the other eight contexts unanswered. Native numerical `predict` on unsupported contexts is **not** treated as a verified intervention; downstream applications must use the exported source-local `choose_certified` readout. Neither native cold restoration nor unverified prior state can silently expand that certification.

The adapter preserves an explicit witnessed-source digest, 32-effect budget, certified coordinate/context counts, half-observed and completely absent coordinates, full original evidence hash, cold native checkpoint and atomic prior conflict checks. Prior checkpoints may be extended, never mutated in place.

## Source-free controlled exercise

- **24 new artificial worlds** (`WSP156|FAKE|` namespace), each independently evaluated with 32 *actually materialized* source-free do effects for each of three policies; total **2,304 charged fixture effects**.
- 12 ON nuisance-invariant worlds; 12 OFF worlds with a nuisance-dependent context that may be **unseen** at the paid budget. A hidden mismatch that was never paired must remain undiagnosed rather than being falsely labeled global invariance.
- Three frozen policies: source-blind SHA-priority eight complete quartets; **strong fixed-balanced** eight quartets at contexts 0–7; SHA-priority uniformly selected 32 of 64 distinct actions. All obtain exactly 32 effects per world. The first two have equal information and must **tie** at eight verified contexts in all 12 ON worlds.
- Exact independent dictionary of the same paid observations checks every native certified choice and every witnessed contradiction/rejection. No counterfactual action is invented. Frozen source-free diagnostic asks whether paired action scheduling yields ≥4 more certified ON contexts per world than SHA-random while **not** outperforming the well-designed fixed-balanced control.
- A single witnessed nuisance disagreement rejects the candidate. A mismatch at an *unobserved* coordinate is a constructive non-identifiability limit, not evidence of model safety.

Passing tests would establish **bounded evidence acquisition and native readout discipline only**, not unfamiliar causal inference, real-world learning, autonomous relationship discovery, calibrated uncertainty, full knowledge population or superiority over independent symbolic memory / fully trained conventional models. The experiment deliberately never predicts held unseen coordinates. Whole-process cost, data budget and model size are recorded as development diagnostics; any genuine efficiency or cognitive gain requires a later independently controlled prospective experiment.
