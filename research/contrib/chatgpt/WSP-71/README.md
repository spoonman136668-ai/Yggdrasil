## Falsifiable hypothesis
A fast neural learner constructs experience-derived hidden weights, stores acquired knowledge only in a frozen numeric model snapshot, and later teaches a separate durable neural student using **bounded, synthetic, self-generated public action/consequence memories**. This may preserve earlier correct predictions after learning from later real observations better than equally compute-bounded duplication of later observations, without storing old training labels in the candidate student.

## Physics, old knowledge and new experience
- Fresh SELFTEST-only seeds **4501–4512** (four source-private role-permuted worlds each of parity/XOR, three-way conjunction, mixed negation/coupling), plus **4513–4514** four-variable parity negative controls. Same broad mechanism families as WSP70, new independently SHA256-salted source; **not** genuinely unfamiliar physics-family transfer.
- **One** physically continuous 192-action no-reset source session per world from all-visible-zero; all 192 actions charged. Phase A is 96 actions, Phase B a further 96 real consequences under the **same fixed source laws** (a change in experienced trajectories, not a change in underlying world). Source/private roles and withheld labels never imported into the learning module.
- Train unchanged source-blind WSP70 `ReusableInteractionNeural` for exactly the first 96 charged actions, with its original 48/96 milestones constructing up to 24 actual hidden ReLU neurons from public residuals and directly editing readout weights. Freeze a numerical, ledger-free matrix/bias/covariance snapshot of this Stage-A teacher.
- The learner may enumerate the 64 *public hypothetical* input state/action pairs **without querying physical source**. Prefer Phase-A previously uncharged public pairs, use only the frozen teacher's confident four-bit next-state outputs, and retain up to **32** pseudo-labeled proposed memories (or fewer when it abstains). Each teacher query, output, and SHA256 pseudo-label receipt is recorded; their eventual physical correctness is audited evaluator-only **after** all students are trained.
- To enforce matched extra weight updates, limit all arms to **k = minimum of 32, number of confident teacher proposals, number of distinct actually charged Phase-A public contexts**. One learner sees exactly the 96 physical Phase-B rows plus k proposed synthetic rows; every matched replay control sees 96 Phase-B rows plus k of its own type of rehearsal. **Pseudo-labels are not fresh physical evidence**, and the candidate has information from its teacher that B-repeat does not. This information advantage is explicitly separate from compute parity.

## Numerical consolidation substrate
- Reuse the **identical frozen learned hidden ReLU feature bank** from the Stage-A teacher (source-independent initial 32 plus up to 24 directly constructed neural hidden weight/bias vectors). The Stage-B durable student resets only its four neural readout vectors and posterior covariance, then writes readout coefficients numerically with bounded rank-one recursive ridge updates (λ=0.5) from 96 new public physical consequences, followed by k synthetic or control rehearsal examples.
- The durable student does **not** import the source generator, read heldout physics, look up symbolic source rules, backpropagate gradients, or acquire free physical transitions during sleep. It retains no Phase-A raw experience in its cold snapshot; prediction uses only numeric hidden weights, readout and posterior covariance. Confidence uses unmodified WSP70 criteria: each bit's output margin ≥0.27 and leverage ≤2.0.
- **Memory-side limitations:** the candidate's dream selection does consult the set of Phase-A previously charged *input identities* to prioritize hypotheticals, but not their old consequence labels. This is auxiliary episodic metadata; don't claim a purely weight-only end-to-end system without it. The hidden feature bank itself is supplied from Stage A and is not adapted in Phase B.

## Matched and stronger controls
- Primary **B-repeat**: same frozen Stage-A hidden bank, 96 identical real Phase-B observations plus exactly k independently seeded duplicates of Phase-B observations. Same rank-one updates, same output confidence, same teacher construction overhead.
- **Stored actual Phase-A replay**: identical B training and k distinct **true physically charged A examples** kept as an old raw buffer. This stronger information control does not obey the candidate's Phase-A label memory restriction, so it is never used as an allegedly equal-information comparison.
- Diagnostic **B-only** with only 96 real Phase-B rows (lower compute, never a primary matched-compute comparator). Negative **scrambled dream** rotates public action channels while retaining teacher pseudo-labels, exposing contamination risks.
- Frozen Stage-A teacher and exact state/action evidence lookup. Separately implemented full **end-to-end gradient-trained 8→32→4 neural MLP** with three Adam restarts on exactly the B-phase physical 96 public observations. It must reach ≥95% training-side accuracy to count as converged; comparison is not same total information because it has no Phase-A teacher.

## Frozen scoring and stop rules
- Evaluate 12×64 = **768** primary evaluator-only physical state/action consequences, separately identifying never-physically-charged contexts after both phases; evaluate 12×32 = **384** separately sampled two-action compositions. Audit retention of **Stage-A confidently correct** physical predictions under every later student.
- At least **260 confident correct** primary predictions, **+20** over matched B-repeat, **≥82%** Stage-A retention and **+15 retained** versus B-repeat, **≥18** confident correct genuinely never charged, **≤24** confident wrong, **≥100** confident correct two-step compositions, **≥85%** true teacher-generated pseudo labels when checked evaluator-only, dreams in ≥10/12 primary worlds, cold matrix-only restore and tamper rejection in all 12, total candidate teacher+selection+update+evaluation CPU ≤2× same teacher+matched B-repeat+evaluation, and ≤6 wrong-confident results in two 4-bit parity negative worlds. **Every** condition is mandatory to get `SELFTEST_SIGNAL`.
- **Any failure** leaves `SELFTEST_NEGATIVE_OR_INCONCLUSIVE`; no post-result changes to physics, frozen seeds, parameters, thresholds, dream count policy or control protocols. All useful negative observations are preserved.
