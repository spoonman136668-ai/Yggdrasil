# CAP04 — causal action-history source identifiability repair

Engineering-only source repair based on the independent prior audit recorded on [PR #1324](https://github.com/spoonman136668-ai/Yggdrasil/pull/1324#issuecomment-7012331245). The earlier source blob `28240be43e7981e1d47100a3a886d04da3c09304` cycles four interventions at a constant phase offset. That construction **fails** to make the previous action distinguishable from the current action and is kept as a historical negative result.

## Source-only change

The prospective engine's two explicit engineering identities now each receive an exogenous four-event block shuffle, generated by SHA-256 from a domain-separated source identity and block number **before** observing state, consequence, hidden world law, or learner forecast. Each block contains each of the four interventions exactly once. The independent source schedule does not change any previously witnessed episodes or trained native/JEPA state. This is not a learned policy or an action-selection controller.

New negative and support tests check: (a) the original four-cycle is provably aliased, (b) every current intervention in the novel 40-event phase occurs with multiple genuine predecessors, (c) multiple identical public-state/current-action pairs occur with distinct predecessors, and (d) two fixture worlds sometimes share the same public clock and current action while having different previous actions. These are **necessary engineering diagnostics**, not proof of causal effect, sufficient novelty, or learned causal-history benefit.

## Fences and validity

No registered DEV/pilot/primary seed was generated, examined, scored, or trained. `generate_registered` remains unconditionally fail-closed. The upstream CAP04 prereg, original CAP03 training/source and both native + six JEPA-head checkpoints remain untouched. The fixed generator is not yet authorized for registered first use.

**Scientific design risks remain:** actual public observations and hidden latent variables may be confounded. Even after action-clock disambiguation on two engineering fixtures, an action-only or clock/history-sham control and full conventional baselines must falsify shortcuts on the prospectively registered cohorts *after* independent first-use authorization. This source repair does not demonstrate learning improvement or generalization.

This branch is isolated from the active PR and accepted main. Engineering CI qualification on this exact candidate plus source-pinned G29 and final two-OS regression is required before adoption.
