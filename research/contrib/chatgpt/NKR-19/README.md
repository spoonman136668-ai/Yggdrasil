API, merge, accepted-model activation or historical-image scan.

identical 24-presentation budgets/world. Replay event identities are counted

persistence and chance controls use the same public prefixes. Another screen
predicts before updating on each post-warmup event. Full predictions, exact
Brier fractions, cold retention and contradictions are preserved whether the
forecast hypothesis succeeds or fails. No neural or policy-value claim.

then generates the standard source atlas and runs mandatory full inherited root,

the generated final candidate. No original experiment is replayed during final
qualification. Original transcripts remain in their existing Actions artifact.
