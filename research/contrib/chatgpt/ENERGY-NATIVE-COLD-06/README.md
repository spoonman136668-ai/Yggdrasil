## Why this replaced another synthetic mechanism sweep

Three consecutive source-free causal-flow / shift research comparisons have not demonstrated superiority over strong conventional Bayesian controls. Rather than retune consumed experiments, this gate audits a *real existing native memory component*, `yggdrasil/public_causal_cells.py`, using the exact accepted Git file.

The component explicitly identifies itself as a **simple fixed-budget counter control**, not as novel intelligence or an intervention-aware learner. It has seven active addresses, nine separately retained addresses, two actions and two public outcomes per address, and a versioned integrity-checked **164-byte** snapshot. There is no hidden-outcome oracle.

Four invalid observation inputs must fail without mutating the source's existing checkpoint. Only local temporary files are used; no owner runner, CI workflow, scheduler, credentials, model checkpoint, original experimental seed or external service is touched.

## What is learned—and not learned

The **snapshot/cold restore actually works on this accepted component**, unlike merely round-tripping JSON in memory. The proof is a fresh operating-system process reading the stored native artifact, checking the correct source identity and reproducing behavior for all 16 slots.

But its historical retention has a strict **address-scoped isolation boundary**, not a general cumulative cognitive memory mechanism:

- Nine retained addresses survive because they were not updated.
- Seven active addresses all flip their old decisions under contrary new observations. Their old relationship was **not independently accessible after the new update**, since the counter has no context/episode lineage.
- This is expected behavior for counters accumulating contradictory outcomes, **not a newly discovered software bug**.
- Comparing action-labeled correlations is **not** proof of causal interventions, counterfactual reasoning, historical episode retrieval, unfamiliar-world transfer or durable cumulative intelligence.
- This test does not touch the Amalgam learner/checkpoint and cannot qualify or disqualify the owner's independently evolving native system. It is a **baseline native control audit**, not an Amalgam acceptance test.

The important successor scientific hypothesis is **context-addressable preservation of an old learned causal function while a second contradictory one is learned**, with authentic interventions and compositional unfamiliar relationships. The strongest comparisons would be equal-information and equal-capacity context-indexed conventional memory, fully fitted dynamic Bayes, a learned structural predictor and exact symbolic oracle, with *real independent-process* cold evaluation of both old and new tasks. That is a **new proposed experiment**, not frozen or executed here, and requires an isolated native interface study and a fresh precommitted cohort.
