## Training-only feature selection and exact weight programming

Within the 48 FIT source-free paired states:
- Fit an eight-feature *NULL* exact-rational ridge (\`lambda=1/64\`) to the first 36 paired public outcomes.
- Independently fit seven nine-feature exact-rational ridge programs on **the same 36 public training outcomes**, adding each candidate feature separately.
- Score **only the remaining 12 FIT selection pairs**. Choose the lowest-RMSE candidate with fixed candidate-order ties if it improves the eight-feature NULL by at least 0.003 RMSE. Otherwise select NONE.
- AFTER that feature choice, refit exactly **one eight/nine-feature direct rank-one rational coefficient matrix on all 48 FIT pairs**, frozen public order. No held states are read for selection or fitting.
