# WSP-125 — Residual-triggered growth of numerical relationship features

**ENGINEERING-ONLY; no original first-use, scientific admission, native merge or deployment.**

## Scientific question

WSP-124 (draft #1136) reconstructed sixteen separate unknown thresholded
causal corrections, but its XOR, threshold-rectangle grammar and binary
search were already supplied, and the strongest symbolic solver tied it.
WSP-113 (draft #1082) earlier solved dense intervention graphs perfectly
in the original synthetic cohort **alongside exact Bayesian, symbolic and
trivial passive controls**. Those original cohorts and negatives are not
replayed or silently counted as new evidence.

WSP-125 changes the representational question. It starts with **only a
known baseline effect**, `left XOR right`. After a genuinely paid source
effect contradicts the baseline, it allocates a NEW numerical relation
feature containing two initially unknown support masks and an unknown
three-bit effect correction. The source law's support sets are arbitrary
subsets of eight typed inputs, cardinality 3..6; unlike WSP-124 they are
**not thresholded rectangles** and are not preprogrammed as correct masks.
The simple low-rank conjunction `left_in_support AND right_in_support`
remains a **provided generic compositional template**: this experiment
does *not* invent all of its own computational primitives.

## Frozen protocol and fairness

`prereg.json` was committed FIRST at
`f291718dd1f33c2784001403e36c7d30650c110a`, before any generator,
new learner or result. Engineering source is inspectable `source.py`,
not a separately sealed evaluator.

- **128 independent hidden relation laws** (fit IDs 125900..126027);
  eight left and eight right typed relation inputs each, 64 possible
  action consequences; source budget **32 distinct paid interventions
  per law = 4,096 source calls per arm**.
- Candidate action policy is outcome-blind until the first anomalous effect.
  It then probes the witness row and column to determine which other typed
  values belong to the new relation. Unknown support bits **abstain**.
  Each allocated relation is 17 int32 slots, 68 bytes **excluding** event
  ledger, Python runtime, checkpoint, source and scoring costs.
- Held fit score = **128×32=4,096 unqueried pairs**. Independent
  unfamiliar-identity transfer = **256 worlds×64=16,384 pairs** at ZERO
  extra source calls; these reuse the 128 source laws under newly randomized
  opaque IDs and are NOT 256 new law discoveries.
- Off-family counterexample = **64 different opaque-identity worlds×64=
  4,096 pairs** with an added unrepresented odd×odd interaction. Report
  confident wrong and abstention honestly; no exposed negative retuning.
- `STRONG_SYMBOLIC_MATCHED` is an **independent full exact version space**
  over every two 8-position support sets of cardinality 3..6 and every
  effect correction 1..7, filtering 308,700 candidate law hypotheses
  from the *same exact source observations and actions* as the numerical
  candidate. It abstains unless its surviving posterior hypotheses
  unanimously entail an outcome. Neither its information nor capacity
  is intentionally weakened. `STRONG_SYMBOLIC_PASSIVE` uses the same
  full hypothesis class and 32 independently random paid actions.
  Fixed-original and no-update are disjoint negative controls.
- Direct numerical state is allocated only after a measured contradiction;
  SHA-256 consistency-checked snapshots replay every evidence event
  and reject digest-repaired fabricated coefficient changes. Cold restore,
  previous-law retention and no cross-law overwrite are mandatory.

## Falsifiability and limits

The frozen development threshold is >=95% held accuracy and zero
confident-wrong within the rank-one source family, including zero-new-action
transfer. Strongest symbolic may be equal or better: **a tie means no unique
numerical learning advantage**. A development NO-GO is a legitimate and
preserved result. The independent off-family evidence explicitly tests
whether this dynamically grown representation still extrapolates
confidently when its generic conjunction assumption is wrong.

No fully trained conventional neural baseline, independent original
scientific source, blind novel primitive invention, replicated
whole-process cost, energy/RSS measurement, robust unknown-physics transfer,
accepted model update or `SCIENTIFIC_PASS` is available. Source-original
125000..125063 and reserved protected cohorts remain DENIED, along with
