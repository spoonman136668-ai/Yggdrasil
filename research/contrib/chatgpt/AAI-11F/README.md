# AAI-11F: AAI-11P benchmark exact-goal feasibility audit

**Finding under qualification:** the original AAI-11P synthetic causal benchmark has a *mathematically unreachable admission criterion*. The previous AAI-11A, 11B, 11D developer tests required >=60% exact three-mediator goal completion, yet the original three mechanical fixture contexts (C/D/E/F causal laws, each N2/N4 exogenous challenge budget, twelve fixed x/goal test contexts per law and fixture) allow at most **144 exact completions out of 288 independently restored two-action evaluation episodes = 50.0%**, even with **perfect knowledge of the hidden laws and an exhaustive optimal 0–2-do-action policy**.

The benchmark's old raw-reward oracle is still 2.448263889 on these contexts. The unreachable success objective must not be interpreted as poor candidate learning or hidden causal inference. The source-level problem is the causal mediator constraints plus the 2-action limit: half the original requested three-bit goals are unreachable regardless of weights, planner, learning, observation history or tokenization. Merely using a stronger neural architecture cannot exceed this ceiling on the frozen task. **This audit is not scientific first-use and does not update Yggdrasil's native architecture.**
