# ENERGY-48 — Do-intervention selection: contradiction-energy vs conventional strategies

**Date:** November 18, 2026. **Classification: DEVELOPMENT_SCREEN_NO_GO.** This is an early-stop **screen**, not an independently preregistered 36-world primary scientific evaluation. No native Yggdrasil model was trained or modified.

## Question and protocols

Can allocating **24 adaptive, previously unseen do-interventions** after 48 initial new-context witnessed outcomes improve cold action-consequence prediction relative to equal-label random selection and conventional information acquisition? The source generates **12-bit randomized interventions** on a five-factor causal consequence system: three persistent, one old-only, one new-only mechanism. New-context nuisance cues reverse correlations and are independently randomized at cold evaluation. Learners see old 96 and new 48 witnessed examples, and the seven selectors receive only unlabeled new-context **192-action pools**. Each chooses **24 actions** before any selected labels are exposed. All variants then train fully on equal **96 old + 72 new = 168 labeled intervention consequences per world**, with completely disjoint 128 old, 128 new, and 128 cold held interventions. The 364-function parity/AND grammar is pre-authored, not discovered; this tests only an artificial small causal world.

Seven acquisition policies: sparse-energy **old-vs-new causal-model disagreement**, dense conventional ElasticNet old-vs-new disagreement, four-member bootstrapped ElasticNet committee variance, Hamming-space diversity, uniformly random acquisition, shuffled-new-label sparse disagreement (negative), and fixed pool-first acquisition. Each acquisition policy is fully evaluated with **two** fully retrained downstream predictors: context-gated cumulative **ElasticNet** and **OMP7**, with identical labels and hyperparameters across policies. Diversity tie handling and selection budgets are frozen in source. The `choose()` entrypoint **does not receive** true factor identities, pool consequence labels, or held outcomes; the simulator reveals the selected consequence labels only after all selectors commit their choices.

**Stop candidate at DEVELOPMENT_SCREEN_NO_GO** without retrospective selection/threshold tuning. **Reserved primary seeds 9109, 9127, 9137 were NOT scored** and remain available for a separately preregistered experiment.
