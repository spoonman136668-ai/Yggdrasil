# ENERGY-06 initial implementation: invalid exact reference, aborted without score

The initial preregistration and source were frozen on 2026-11-17 at source SHA-256 `c81943cedf53c661802bf9dbb221082ab0660d7a80e8f31bbee7ebf88c21fc34` and prereg SHA-256 `b776e839858cfcf3f04d52aff0ab12f8e9dc1b0b6a562166c8d6b8bab5658342`. The first scored evaluation stopped with an assertion before emitting `scored.json`. The original scored cohort (seeds 17, 29, 43) is treated as exposed and was not reused for the corrected experiment.

Root cause: SciPy/HiGHS MILP presolve reported status `0` (optimal) while a feasible candidate had a strictly lower objective. On a diagnostic 14-variable case, the solver's returned objective was `-6.6215857129093285` while the independently constructed feasible candidate was `-6.762266373842408`; the reported dual bound matched the latter, despite the returned gap claiming `0`. This also occurred in a no-intervention case, demonstrating that the redundant 1e8 intervention penalty alone was not the entire cause. With `presolve=False`, the diagnostic optimum was correctly `-6.762266373842409`.

The repair was engineering-only and was independently smoke-tested with the non-scoring seed 999 at 14 and 22 variables. The new version `R1` uses fresh seeds 61, 73 and 89, removes the redundant hard-clamp penalty from the MILP objective, disables faulty presolve, and rejects inconsistent reported objective/bound certificates. The original frozen files remain in this package; this is not post-result scientific tuning or evidence of scientific gain. Exact MILP status is further validated by an independent exhaustive 2^14 search on all 72 size-14 scored problems, with zero objective discrepancy.
