# NCI-NOVEL-01 — pre-primary arithmetic amendment

Initial frozen design commit: `697933bb73c97ac2a12ecd3fc7b9c40c3577a666`.

**No code, source generator, reserved development seeds, cohort episodes or primary scores were executed between the initial design and this amendment.**

The initial design counted eight mechanism families and five phases totaling 288 episodes per family. With two actual interventions per episode, its proposed per-arm/per-seed budget of 3,840 actions was inconsistent with the existing phase roster:

`(4 novel + 2 unchanged + 1 identifiability negative + 1 out-of-class negative) × (64 + 64 + 32 + 64 + 64) × 2 = 4,608` source-free action slots.

The **only scientific design change** before first primary use is raising the action cap to **4,608** per arm per seed to match those already specified stages. The generator, candidate, sealed cohorts and first scored results remain **NOT RUN**; this is neither post-result tuning nor source-owner authorization. This amendment is a separate append-only source-custody receipt. Full end-to-end matched compute/action/cost remains a hard qualification requirement.
