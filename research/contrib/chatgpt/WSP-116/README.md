## Why this gate matters

WSP-110's million fact weights were **92.5% distractors**. A real knowledge graph with 20M+ interrelated retained facts tests a materially different proposition: whether *breadth of interconnected evidence* improves unfamiliar association answers. It can falsify that limited proposition on historical consumed data without falsely claiming a native-intelligence breakthrough.

Even a successful dose-response is **not independent `SCIENTIFIC_PASS`**. It cannot establish causal discovery or that weight programming beats exact symbolic storage. The subsequent worthwhile scientific experiment requires **unopened unseen relation/family cohorts**, real novel relation semantics and a fully trained conventional model with identical information/actions/whole-process compute. Retain this distinction in any report.
