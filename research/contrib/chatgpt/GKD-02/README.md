## Why this is distinct

This study starts from the donor's *conditional* temperature rule `T=E/(m*c)` and deliberately exposes contexts where only an unknown fraction of supplied energy becomes heat. The candidate's numeric memory may correct a context-local coefficient **only from witnessed outcomes**. One contradictory witness triggers abstention, two agreeing contradictions can revise, and two incompatible witnesses prohibit confident admission. Other contexts and cold serialization must remain stable.

A small, self-contained synthetic evaluator implements four contexts, two observation phases, one deliberately corrupted witness, and a separate nonlinear-law incompatibility test. Heldout consequences are computed independently from test-only hidden context gains; no actual electrical/thermal world, original causal research source, protected first use, model teacher call or GPT weights are used.
