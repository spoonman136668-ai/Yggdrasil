# WSP-129 — hypothesis-conditioned causal operator discovery

**Research track: direct numerical weight programming, development-only.**
**No protected first-use. No native Yggdrasil admission. No merge to accepted main.**

## Why this experiment exists

WSP-127's adaptive consequence acquisition increased coverage by +32.93 percentage points against a matched fixed intervention policy, but failed its 95% accuracy requirement and its unfamiliar-law safety negative. WSP-128's feedback-gated numerical repair achieved 88.74% correct withheld rank-nine consequences, but was given the single-rank-one residual template and tied an independent exact symbolic comparator. Neither demonstrates invention of an unfamiliar relationship structure.

WSP-129 asks a narrower but falsifiable question: **can a source-blind numerical bank infer from consequences which composition of operators and hidden parity masks governs a world, and refuse unsupported predictions if that world's causal equation is outside its available compositional grammar?**

This is not open-ended algebra invention: the operator library {XOR, AND, OR}, 4-bit parity features, and 1,350 possible candidate compositions are available before any training. The specific causal law, masks, source family and evaluation outcomes are not supplied to learners. A source-only two-composition negative tests what the bank **cannot** discover.

## Frozen before implementation

The only first WSP-129 commit was `5eb6addded6fd82b251e3389a1a87d412bcf1ff2`, containing `prereg.json` alone. Exact WSP-128 parent `d6090288322e58b4e65da3618d344b012947f178` is engineering-qualified, but its scientific claim remains mixed. This contribution is outside Yggdrasil's accepted model.

- 128 deterministic worlds with 16×16 typed binary consequence interventions, source family withheld from models and scrambled independently of public law identity: 96 on-grammar (32 each XOR/AND/OR) and 32 two-composition off-grammar.
- Each world receives precisely **24 paid actions per arm**, leaving 232 withheld consequences. A source-only oracle labels off-grammar worlds; no model sees the labels.
- The numerical candidate writes a packed 1,350-bit hypothesis-weight vector and eliminates incompatible parameterized compositions from actual effects. Feedback-driven intervention selection maximizes disagreement among remaining hypotheses. If no supported program remains, it abstains.
- Independently implemented complete symbolic grammar enumerator observes the SAME adaptive transcript. An optimized fixed-design symbolic comparator and numerical twin receive the 24 prespecified fixed probes. A globally converged, convex 25-parameter bilinear ridge regression is fitted to the same 24 adaptive consequences. No-update abstains, as a deliberately limited negative.
- **Zero-source relabel transfer:** 32 new subject-ID maps of previously learned on-grammar laws, all 256 predictions/view, with cold exact state restoration after later learning and tamper detection.
- Frozen primary: >=95% held accuracy across 96 on-grammar worlds, zero confident wrong, >=90% correct operator-family identification, within 2 percentage points of fixed optimized symbolic. Unfamiliar two-composition negative: >=90% of 32 worlds eliminate ALL known single-operator candidates and <=1% confident wrong among 7,424 withheld decisions.
- The exact independent symbolic comparator is the strongest required *grammar-matched* control. Numerical ties cannot prove new physics, weight-space advantage, AGI, or efficient general intelligence. The fitted ridge control is **not** a fully trained deep neural comparator, so a separate sealed science qualification is required.
