# CKAD25-X — active discrimination from persistent native causal memory

**Source-frozen, source-free prospective research.** No accepted native model, owner route, protected first-use, FORGE dispatch or autonomous controller is touched.

## Why this is a distinct gate

CKAD25-W #1251 was engineering-qualified but found unlabelled known-causal recall in **only one of two synthetic seeds**; its fixed four target probes were uninformative in the other. CKAD35 #1252 tested conventional donor-analogy transfer, **failed its frozen source-family threshold**, and proposed active probes against ambiguity. CKAD34 #1250 already tested source-selected polynomial class. CKAD25-X does **not** re-run those labels, optimize W's 0.90 gate, modify W's retrieved old examples, or invent another substrate.

This tests whether the existing `NativeCumulativeLearner` can select **actions whose consequences distinguish its two *already-learned* numeric causal memories**, as new unlabelled worlds arrive. The candidate `propose_causal_discriminator` is an opt-in, **read-only** function on the SAME cumulative checkpoint, not a new learner, queue or scheduled actor. Its supplied unknown-world p=.5, source-conditioned committee, and exact-rational between-class disagreement (Gini-information proxy) remain **conventional prior and decision assumptions**, not self-discovered causal primitives.

### Exact frozen go/no-go

Primary metrics: held Brier (lower better, p=.5 for abstention), native familiar-phase/returned A classification, answered/incorrect, unfamiliar C/D confident harm, **native exact-conventional posterior parity at each probe and held prediction**, memory bytes/old source retention, per-seed generalization. A qualifying development signal requires **all** of the preregistered gates:

- Mean candidate held Brier ≥0.04 better than both fixed and random under equal actions.
- Correct returned-A in ≥3/4 independent synthetic seeds and known A/B/A in ≥9/12 phase cases.
- ≥20/48 confident known-phase held answers, ≤3 wrong; unknown C/D Brier ≤0.27 and ≤1 confident wrong.
- Candidate Brier within +0.03 of conventional EIG native recall, within +0.03 of best fully fitted logistic/forest, and within +0.04 of exact Bayesian continuous mixture trained on candidate source/diagnostics.
- Original native and both source-conditioned causal committees preserved after P/Q and cold restore; no action leakage or unequal budget; independently recomputed exact native/proposer and hard-readout parity for every query.

A miss is **DEVELOPMENT_NO_GO** (or INVALID on protocol drift), not post-hoc tuning. Even a 100% gate pass is **not SCIENTIFIC_PASS**: a conventional 697-hypothesis grammar, prior/unknown alternative and Gini choice are supplied, and source-free finite GF2 worlds do not establish open-world causality or GI. No native promotion/merge/deployment.
