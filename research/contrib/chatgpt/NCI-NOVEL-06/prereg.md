# NCI-NOVEL-06 — Causal operator consolidation and compositional transfer: frozen prospective protocol

**Protocol freeze date:** 2026-11-20. **Stage:** PREREGISTERED SOURCE DESIGN ONLY; no implementation, pilot, training, first-use or scored evidence. This is an **independent contributor** follow-up under existing Yggdrasil [PR #1345](https://github.com/spoonman136668-ai/Yggdrasil/pull/1345), not a new cumulative learner. The single accepted integration coordinator remains [Amalgam PR #1278](https://github.com/spoonman136668-ai/Yggdrasil/pull/1278).

## Why this differs from NCI-NOVEL-05

NCI-NOVEL-05's final candidate predictions are exactly those of FULL-BMA after expansion, or BASE-BMA otherwise: it merely selects existing, fully fitted models; [structural proof](https://github.com/spoonman136668-ai/Yggdrasil/pull/1345#issuecomment-7047856319). Its original four primary seeds and its later 21 engineering worlds are consumed/exposed after aborted executions and MUST NEVER be replayed or recycled. Do not reinterpret NCI05 incomplete primary as a scored scientific failure.

This successor does **not** test another threshold that switches between fixed <=2 and <=4-degree two-monomial BMA. Instead it proposes a **learned, persistent operator library** in a tokenless causal-state substrate: infer small intervention-conditioned causal primitives from public action/outcome witnesses, retain them, construct **new causal compositions** from these learned primitives under contradictions, and store only the revised bindings. A new composition must change the represented predictive function, rather than choosing an existing pre-fitted BMA readout. Stored source law labels or direct weight writing do not count as learning.

## Falsifiable hypothesis

Under a fixed byte/compute/observation budget and identical public action opportunities, a learner that (a) induces reusable intervention-effect operators, (b) assembles an unseen **three-operator** causal DAG from witnessed contradictions, and (c) preserves pre-existing operator readouts after updating only the new bindings will attain lower held-out predictive error and higher intervention value on **unfamiliar compositions** than the strongest equally informed fully trained conventional, exact-symbolic and Bayesian controls. No claim of intelligence, novelty or transfer follows from expressivity alone.

This is distinct from issue #1088's broader foundational-knowledge program: it isolates **whether actual learned operator reuse** provides information beyond a fully fitted symbolic composition and a same-information shuffled library, without preloading a knowledge graph or operating the accepted native learner. Reuse existing issue #1088 and independent Amalgam ownership; do not establish another research coordinator.

## Source-free test design (freeze before any executable source)

- Inputs: five **public** binary state bits plus one binary intervention action; one binary consequence with iid 0.06 Bernoulli label noise. Exogenous evaluation action/outcome random stream must be independent of every arm's policy and identical across arms.
- Primitive discovery: 12 distinct training worlds with 128 publicly witnessed action/consequence tuples per world. Primitive basis is unknown to all learners; no law identity, true term, source family label, noise draw, held answer or source-local hidden state is passed to any arm. Each arm sees the same world key, public inputs, action/outcome records, order and training budget.
- Unfamiliar transfer: four composition families combining three previously learned operators with a previously unseen permutation, connector or action-conditional binding. At least one genuine target per family must be **outside** the full truth-function set of the original NCI05 two-monomial <=4-degree ANF grammar; confirm this with an **independent exhaustive 32-input truth-table membership audit** before outcome sampling. The held-generating DAG is unknown to the candidate. A negative world with no new operator, one with an unidentifiable effect under public observations, and one using a genuinely outside-library primitive are also mandatory.
- Episodes: after primitive training, each held composition world has 160 fresh public intervention witnesses (first 96 for online fit, last 64 for frozen, train-only model/selector choice); then 128 held shared-action predictions, 128 paired-policy action choices on common exogenous potential outcomes, and 128 earlier primitive-world old-return queries. HELD and old-return **never train** the learner. Perform A -> B -> A return; checkpoint-before/after plus cold process restore; compute functional Brier, not just identical serialized bytes.
- Developer engineering diagnostics may use ONLY distinct, separately registered engineering fixtures and must NOT access these scored-world outcomes. Freeze a new exact pre-execution source SHA, grammar, code, seeds, splits and source/owner custody before a single prospective first use. Any first-use abort consumes all potentially exposed cohort IDs; no automatic retry.

## Compared arms and fairness

1. **CAND:** bounded causal-operator library, causal composition inferred exclusively from public witnessed interventions, contradiction-triggered new binding, old primitives preserved. No target truth term, oracle family label or hidden causal graph admitted.
2. **FLAT-BMA:** NCI05-style fully fitted degree <=4 two-monomial Bayesian average, with **no adaptive threshold**; explicitly report its expressivity ceiling.
3. **FULL-COMP-BAYES:** independent **exact compositional grammar/Bayesian inference** over all allowable three-operator binding DAGs with the same primitive hypothesis class, evidence and priors; may use more compute, which must be disclosed. This is the primary exact-symbolic strong control, not a deliberately weakened base.
4. **MATCHED-COMP-SEARCH:** fully fitted compositional program induction under an equal per-world byte/compute limit and equal prior information. Must search the complete same admissible candidate class under that limit.
5. **TRAINED-CONVENTIONAL:** independently fitted ExtraTrees, boosted-tree and MLP or recurrent predictor tuned **only** on designated training/validation data; train to convergence or a predeclared, identical explicit budget; report curves. Do not use undertrained baselines.
6. **ABLATIONS:** no retained operator library, random/shuffled primitive bindings with identical capacity, same learned primitives but no contradiction-triggered composition, and no online adaptation.
7. **NO-LEARNING / ORACLE-REFERENCE:** fixed prior and evaluator-only source oracle, reported separately. The oracle is a ceiling, never a competitor eligible for an ordinary learned-control superiority claim.

All arms must receive identical total public interventions, side information, source-world training, target-world fit/validation/held data and action opportunities. Account for primitive foundation construction, fitting, selection, adaptation, forecast, cold restore, storage bytes, peak RSS, CPU, wall time and action selection. Policy outcomes must use fixed exogenous potential-outcome keys for both actions, without giving counterfactual outcomes to a learner.

## Frozen scoring and honest gates

- **Minimum primary cohort:** four genuinely untouched preregistered independent seeds, each with all seven target/negative families. Distinct source-first and exactly-once custody must be independently verified before use; no seeds from NCI05 (`809001,809011,809033,809057,809089,917201,917203,917207`) may be used. **No prospective seed is reserved/authorized by this protocol document**; an owner-gated, source-pinned sealed seed manifest must be committed *before implementation and any first run*, not invented after viewing held outcomes.
- For the four novel families independently, across all four seeds, candidate must improve **HELD_SHARED Brier by >=5% relative** and **HELD_POLICY reward by >=0.02 absolute** against the best competently fitted non-oracle control chosen solely by VALID, with >=3/4 novel families meeting **both** conditions. Also report paired mean/95% CIs and exact-symbolic gap, whether or not the family gate passes. If FULL-COMP-BAYES is the validation winner and candidate ties it, this family **cannot** pass the strict predictive gate.
- Old functional readout: after learning the new composition and after a cold restart, every seed's mean earlier primitive task Brier must regress by **no more than +0.02 absolute** relative to its frozen pre-B checkpoint; older trained/checkpoint state and source proofs must remain accessible and unchanged.
- Negatives: **0/4 false novel-operator identifications** per negative family (no new-law, unidentifiable, outside-library). Abstention is acceptable when the evidence is insufficient. A learner that predicts by trivially caching source identifiers, leak labels or directly installing oracle operators is disqualified.
- Diagnostic: at least **one predicted intervention contrast outside NCI05's fixed function class** must be supported by a held witnessed outcome AND have lower expected held Brier than the fixed-class model. Merely enumerating a larger grammar or predicting a deliberately selected nonmember function is **NOT** proof of learned discovery.
- **Decision:** `DEV_GO` only when all above prespecified tests pass and measured whole-process cost stays inside the frozen cohort budget (no hidden unfair extra compute). Otherwise `DEV_NO_GO`, or `UNVERIFIED` if execution incomplete. No post-result reruns, threshold tuning or selective trial discard.
- Any engineering efficiency improvement (>=2x time, <=50% bytes at <=0.01 held Brier/reward cost) is reported **separately** as `EFFICIENCY_ONLY`; it cannot substitute for scientific superiority over the strongest control.
- Native `SCIENTIFIC_PASS` requires an independently reproduced, original-source, matched-information result and separate owner acceptance; dual-OS CI, design documents, structural expressivity, synthetic source-only scores, native checkpoint bytes and engineering PASS are not scientific proof.
