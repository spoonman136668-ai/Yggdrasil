## Mechanism hypothesis, explicitly testable

The existing bridge learns an immediate reward readout for each action from its current public observation and own original eight-cell neighbor features. In a two-step task where the second reward is caused by the **first selected action** as well as the second, that bridge presently trains the delayed consequence on the *second public observation `Nothing`* without preserving the first action/observation in the learned input. We will test whether a **bounded eight-cell source-public temporal eligibility vector** feeding the **unchanged native predictor at the delayed step** improves genuine acting learning.

Original local genome K=3/D=7, eight roles, full8 native.observe, neighbor-only graph, native memory, native action-credit updates, true reward labels and source public validations remain unchanged. No additional trainable model weights or source-law/instructor table. Proposed v12 stores **two binary eight-cell snapshots** (public `_temporal_public_bits` phase 0 and 1) of the **last actually selected public (cue, action)** when a legal transition is accepted with `terminal=False` (one eligibility event only). On a subsequent observation `Nothing`, candidate action's `_temporal_public_bits("Nothing",candidate,phase)` is composed with matching-phase retained bits using **per-cell XOR** (no global lookup/answer or new edges) before predicting and training the full original `LocalTaskRuntime`. The delayed source reward +10/-3 is applied only to the actually chosen second action. At second terminal public feedback, eligibility clears, including at frozen inference; on rollback no state changes. Each original fit step remains exactly **one** `native.observe` and **one** neighbor-memory feedback, and full8 supervision remains intact. Checkpoint schema v12 stores bounded two-phase source-public eligibility, fail-closed against invalid values/shape and preserves cold-restart and evaluation/frozen weight identity. Correctness includes input-before-feedback consistency, original frozen mode no trained update, absence of delayed-context state in next episode, and no change to original `yggdrasil/task_runtime.py` or native ring.

This **does not** give the learner true hidden first-action rewards or any evaluator-provided ideal action: all causal attribution must emerge through native fit from its *own* two genuinely chosen public actions, never from unchosen feedback. Per-cell XOR may itself alias combinations; measure representation collisions. No promise of conventional superiority.
