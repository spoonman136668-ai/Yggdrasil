# Yggdrasil — standing engineering and research instructions

## Cognitive North Star

Yggdrasil is an **independent developmental/regenerative cognitive architecture** whose native learned state should eventually support LLM-class or greater general intelligence, including cumulative memory, continual learning, reasoning, compositional transfer, language interaction, adaptation and safeguarded self-maintenance, with materially lower compute and memory requirements where feasible.

**Do not build Yggdrasil's cognitive foundation around a transformer, conventional autoregressive next-token prediction, or a token-centric language model.** Tokens or symbols at I/O boundaries, and conventional pretrained models as bounded scaffolds, training tools, or rigorously matched controls are permissible; they must not supply the claimed deployed native cognition. In particular, DG-1G frozen pretrained seeds and DG-1H adapter comparisons are *experimental controls or intermediate mechanism probes*, not the destination.

Preserve the DG-1 genome/phenotype distinction, local or bounded-neighborhood development, functional specialization, selective repair, regeneration, and native accumulated capability. Wingless shares the capability target but remains a separate architecture with independently qualified evidence. Learned Causal Relational Memory and other designs are research candidates, not prescribed end states.

Prioritize new mechanisms proven in a **working cognitive substrate**, including retention, unfamiliar causal/compositional transfer and measured resource cost; isolated toy scores, regeneration of nonfunctional structure or gate throughput are not sufficient. Maintain frozen preregistrations, independent controls, negative findings, sealed first-use, resource accounting and accepted baseline/source identities. Do not retrospectively rewrite experiment outcomes.
